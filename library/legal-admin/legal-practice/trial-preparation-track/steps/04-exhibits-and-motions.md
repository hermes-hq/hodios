# Step 4: Exhibits and pretrial motions

1. Exhibit list: each exhibit with its number, description, sponsoring witness, foundation needed, likely objection and whether a stipulation could resolve it.
2. Motions to consider: motions in limine to exclude or admit evidence, and other pretrial matters, each with the evidence affected and the argument in one line, marked for attorney decision.
3. Pretrial filings checklist from the pretrial order, with dates to verify.

Sections: Exhibit list, Motions to consider, Pretrial filings, Questions. Stop for approval.
