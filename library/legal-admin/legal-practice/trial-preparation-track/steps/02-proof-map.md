# Step 2: Proof map

1. Break every claim and defence into elements, using elements the attorney supplies or marking them [NEEDED].
2. For each element, list the witnesses and exhibits that prove or rebut it, and any admissibility issue (foundation, hearsay, authentication, privilege) to resolve.
3. Mark elements with thin or no proof and what could close the gap (a witness, a records custodian, a stipulation, a request for admission).

Sections: Proof map (table: Element | Our proof | Their proof | Admissibility issues | Gap), Gaps to close, Questions. Stop for approval.
