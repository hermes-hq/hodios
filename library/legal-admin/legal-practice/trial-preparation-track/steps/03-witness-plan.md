# Step 3: Witness plan

1. Order the witnesses to tell the story clearly, with the elements each one proves.
2. For each witness: direct examination topics or cross-examination goals, exhibits to use, impeachment material, preparation sessions needed, and subpoena or travel needs.
3. Expert witnesses: opinions to be given, disclosure status, and challenges expected.
4. Estimate time per witness against any time limit.

Sections: Witness order (table), Witness notes, Experts, Timing, Questions. Stop for approval.
