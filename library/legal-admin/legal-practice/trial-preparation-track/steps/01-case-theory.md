# Step 1: Case theory

1. State the case theory in three sentences: what happened, why the law entitles the client to win, and why the fact-finder should want that result.
2. Propose a theme of a few words that fits the evidence.
3. List the other side's likely theory and its best facts.
4. List the bad facts for the client and how the theory accounts for each.
5. Note any decisions the attorney must make now (for example claims to drop, stipulations to seek).

Sections: Theory, Theme, Their theory, Bad facts, Decisions, Questions. Stop for approval.
