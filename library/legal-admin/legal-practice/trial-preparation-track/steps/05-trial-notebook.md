# Step 5: Trial notebook

Assemble the trial-day binder plan:
1. Contents with tabs: case theory and theme, proof map, opening outline, witness outlines in order, exhibit list with status, proposed instructions or legal framework, motion rulings, closing outline linked to the proof map.
2. A daily checklist: witnesses confirmed, exhibits copied, equipment, objections log.
3. A closing outline that walks the fact-finder through each element with the evidence that proved it, to update as trial goes on.

Sections: Notebook contents, Daily checklist, Closing outline, Open items.
