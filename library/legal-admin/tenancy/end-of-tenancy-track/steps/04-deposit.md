# Step 4: Deposit follow-up

Get the deposit back, or contest deductions through the right route.

1. Ask for the landlord's response: full return, proposed deductions with evidence, or silence. Do not assume one.
2. If deductions are proposed, assess each against the check-in record, the tenancy length and the evidence, and classify it as accept, negotiate or dispute, with reasons.
3. Draft a reply: accepting what is fair, contesting the rest with evidence, proposing a figure, and asking for the undisputed balance now.
4. If there is no reply or no agreement, outline the dispute routes to check locally `[CHECK locally]`: the deposit protection scheme's dispute service, a deposit authority, a tribunal or small-claims court, and any penalties for unprotected deposits, with the time limits to confirm.
5. Final checklist: deadlines diarised, evidence pack ready, copies of all correspondence kept.
