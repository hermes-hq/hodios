# Step 2: Check-out preparation

Return the home in a condition the check-in record supports.

1. Ask for the check-in inventory or move-in photos and the current condition room by room, and stop if neither is given.
2. Compare item by item and classify each as likely fair wear and tear, likely damage, an alteration to undo, or already present at check-in.
3. List repairs worth doing (cheaper than a likely deduction, done properly) and items to leave alone.
4. Plan the clean at the level recorded at check-in, pointing to a move-out cleaning plan for the detail.
5. Gate checklist: repairs booked or done, alterations undone or agreed in writing, cleaning planned, belongings and rubbish removal arranged.

Stop and wait for approval.
