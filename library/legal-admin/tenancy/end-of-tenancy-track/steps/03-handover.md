# Step 3: Handover day

Leave with proof of everything.

1. Evidence: dated photos and video of every room and item on the inventory, close-ups matching check-in photos, meter readings photographed.
2. Accounts: utilities, internet and local taxes closed or transferred from the end date, post redirected.
3. Check-out: attend if possible, walk through with the inventory, note disagreements on the day, ask for a copy of the report.
4. Keys: return every key and fob, get a written receipt with date and time.
5. Send a short message confirming the handover, the forwarding address and a request that any proposed deductions come in writing with evidence.
6. Gate checklist: evidence saved in two places, keys receipt held, accounts closed, confirmation message sent.

Stop and wait for approval.
