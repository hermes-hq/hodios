# Step 1: Notice

Make sure the tenancy ends on the date the tenant intends.

1. Work out the earliest valid end date from the lease and the likely local rules, showing the calculation and marking legal minimums `[CHECK locally]`. If the tenancy is in a fixed term without a break clause, say notice may not end it early and outline the options (surrender by agreement, replacement tenant).
2. If joint tenants are staying, warn that one tenant's notice may affect everyone and recommend advice first.
3. Draft the notice letter with the end date, forwarding address, request for a check-out appointment, and signatures of all leaving tenants.
4. Explain how to serve it with proof.
5. Gate checklist: end date confirmed, notice sent with proof, landlord's acknowledgement requested, rent payable until the end date diarised.

Stop and wait for approval.
