# Step 1: Data inventory

1. Ask for anything missing that changes the picture: systems and spreadsheets used, vendors, staff and customer counts, countries involved, and any sensitive data (health, children, biometrics, criminal records).
2. Build the register of processing: one row per activity (for example payroll, customer orders, newsletter, CCTV, recruitment) with purpose, people, data categories, source, systems, vendors, recipients, countries, and a retention placeholder.
3. Flag high-risk activities that may need a DPIA.

Sections: Register, High-risk flags, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval and answers.
