# Step 3: Vendors and transfers

1. From the register, list every vendor that processes personal data, its role (processor or controller), whether a data processing agreement is in place, and where data is stored or accessed from.
2. Mark transfers that may need a mechanism and assessment, marked "verify".
3. Prioritise vendors by risk and list what to request from each (agreement, security evidence, subprocessor list).

Sections: Vendor table, Transfers to resolve, Requests to send.

Stop and wait for approval.
