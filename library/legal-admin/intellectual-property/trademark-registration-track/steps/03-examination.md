# Step 3: Examination and opposition

Answer the office and any third party on time.

1. Ask what has happened: nothing yet, an examiner's report, publication, an opposition or a letter from another owner. Do not assume.
2. For an examiner's objection: explain it in plain words, the response options (argue, amend or narrow the goods, add evidence of distinctiveness, divide the application), and the deadline.
3. For an opposition or third-party letter: summarise the earlier right, the overlap, and the options (defend, narrow, coexistence agreement, withdraw), and recommend an attorney.
4. Warn about unsolicited invoices and "registration services" that imitate official notices.
5. Gate checklist: every deadline met or extended, response filed or decision recorded, copies kept.

Stop and wait for approval.
