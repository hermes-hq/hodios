# Step 2: Application

Prepare a filing that matches the business.

1. Confirm the applicant is the right legal entity and that it owns any logo (written assignment from the designer if needed).
2. Choose word mark, logo or both, and explain the trade-off.
3. Draft the goods and services specification by class, specific to real and planned use, marking class numbers and accepted terms for checking.
4. Lay out the filing route options (national, regional, international) and the priority window from a first filing, as checks, with the cost and timing trade-offs from the user's budget.
5. List the use and specimen evidence the office may want.
6. Gate checklist: applicant, mark, specification, route, filing date and receipt saved, every deadline diarised.

Stop and wait for approval.
