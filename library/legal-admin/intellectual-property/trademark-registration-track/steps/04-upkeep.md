# Step 4: Registration and upkeep

Keep the registration alive and useful.

1. Record the registration details and certificate, and how to use the registration symbol correctly where allowed (to check per market).
2. Build a calendar of renewal and any use-declaration windows, marked for checking with the office.
3. Set up an evidence of use routine by goods and market, and note the risk if registered goods go unused.
4. Plan low-cost watching for copycat filings and marketplace use, and what to do when one appears.
5. Note when to extend to new markets or file for a new logo or product line.
6. Final checklist: calendar set, evidence folder started, owner and address on the register correct, someone responsible named.
