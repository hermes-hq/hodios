# Step 3: Requirement gaps

Compare the user's position with each requirement in the official wording they supplied.

1. For each requirement (for example language level, knowledge or civics test, income or employment, accommodation, insurance, tax and social security record, good character), say what the user has, what is missing and what evidence would show it.
2. For gaps that take time (a language exam booking, a knowledge test, a full year of tax records), the lead time and the latest date to start.
3. Any requirement where the user's facts are borderline, flagged for adviser review.

Sections: Requirements (table: Requirement as given | Your position | Gap | Action | Lead time), Borderline points, Questions. Stop for approval.
