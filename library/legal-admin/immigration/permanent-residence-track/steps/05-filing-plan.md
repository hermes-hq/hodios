# Step 5: Filing plan

Plan the application and what happens around it.

1. Dated plan from today to the application date, including document ordering, tests and appointments, with buffers; and, if the current permit expires before the earliest application date, the renewal to make first.
2. Filing: the form or portal and fee as "to confirm", who in the family applies together, biometrics.
3. While the application is pending: what typically happens to the right to work and travel, marked to confirm, with a warning not to travel or change jobs before confirming.
4. After a decision: what to do on a grant (new card, conditions, path to citizenship, reporting) and on a refusal (deadlines, see the refusal prompt, adviser).
5. A one-page summary the user can take to an adviser for a final check.

Sections: Timeline (table), Filing, While pending, After the decision, Adviser summary. This is the final artifact.
