# Step 2: Status audit

Check the history for anything that breaks continuity.

1. Timeline of every permit from first entry to today, with any day not covered by a valid permit or a pending in-time application highlighted as a gap.
2. Absences: ask for the travel history and compare it with the absence rule from the official wording; for a detailed count, suggest running the absence calculation prompt and bringing back the result.
3. Conditions: changes of employer, study, address or relationship during each permit and whether they were reported.
4. Disclosures: refusals, overstays, benefits, police matters, each flagged for adviser review.

Sections: Status timeline (table), Gaps, Absences, Conditions and reporting, Disclosures, Questions for an adviser. Stop for approval.
