# Step 4: Evidence bundle

Plan the documents for the application.

1. For each requirement and for continuity of residence, the documents that usually prove it, who issues them, recency rules, translations or certification needed, all to confirm against the official list.
2. Consistency checks: names, dates and addresses that must match across documents and the form.
3. A bundle index with numbering and file names for online upload, and copies to keep.
4. Short explanatory notes for anything unusual, tied to a document.

Sections: Document tracker (table: Document | Proves | Issued by | Recency | Translation | Status), Consistency checks, Bundle index, Explanatory notes, Questions. Stop for approval.
