# Step 2: Export and consent check

1. List what to export from the current platform: subscribers with sign-up date, source and status (active, unsubscribed, bounced, complained), tags or segments, paid status, posts, images and automations.
2. Check consent: keep only active subscribers who opted in to this newsletter; keep unsubscribed, bounced and complained addresses as a suppression list, never as recipients. Flag any imported or purchased segments for removal.
3. Decide whether to run an inactive clean-up before moving, and why.
4. Plan the data handling: where export files are stored, who has access, and when they are deleted.

Sections: Export list, Consent rules, Clean-up decision, Data handling, Open questions.
