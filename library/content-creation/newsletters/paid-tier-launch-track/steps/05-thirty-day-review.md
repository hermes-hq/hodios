# Step 5: Thirty-day review

Needs the real numbers after 30 days. If they are missing, ask for them and stop; never invent results.

1. Compare actual paid subscribers, conversion, annual versus monthly mix, refunds and cancellations, and free unsubscribes after launch emails with the step 1 range.
2. Check the workload: hours actually spent on paid content against the plan.
3. Read reader replies for what paid readers value and what free readers felt.
4. Recommend at most three changes (offer, cadence, upgrade line, what is free) and the metric to watch for each over the next 60 days.

Sections: Results versus plan (table), Workload, Reader signals, Changes, Open questions.
