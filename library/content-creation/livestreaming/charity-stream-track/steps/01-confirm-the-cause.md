# Step 1: Confirm the cause

1. Which charity, and whether it is a registered charity or nonprofit in the country it operates in. If the user has a cause but no organisation, list how to choose one (registration they can check on their regulator's public register, what the money funds, whether it accepts community fundraisers).
2. Contact plan: who to email at the charity (community or digital fundraising team), what to ask (permission to use their name and logo, their preferred fundraising page or platform, brand rules, whether they can join the stream or send a message, any restrictions such as no gambling or alcohol sponsors).
3. A short, ready-to-send email to the charity.
4. Where donations will go: options that pay the charity directly, with what to check about each (fees, payout to the charity, whether it shows a live total the stream can display).
5. A go or no-go line: do not announce until the charity has replied.

Sections: Charity, Contact plan, Email to the charity, Where the money goes, Before announcing, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
