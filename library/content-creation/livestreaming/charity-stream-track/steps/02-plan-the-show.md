# Step 2: Plan the show

1. Length and shape: a total length the streamer can sustain, with blocks (for example 2 to 3 hours each), planned breaks, a meal break and a hard end time. If they want a very long stream, plan shifts with co-hosts rather than one person awake throughout.
2. Segments: what happens in each block, guests, a moment to explain the cause in the charity's own words, and recurring short reminders of what a donation does (only figures the charity provides).
3. Incentives and milestones: a ladder of donation goals tied to fun, safe things (a game, a challenge, a cosplay, a vote), checked against the rules above. Mark any prize draw or raffle as a check with local rules and the charity, since many places regulate them.
4. Announcement plan: when and where to announce, the first post, and a reminder schedule.
5. Roles: who hosts, who runs the board, who reads donations, who moderates.

Sections: Show shape, Segments, Incentive ladder, Announcement plan, Roles, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
