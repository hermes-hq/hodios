# Step 3: Set up donations and safety

1. Donation setup checklist: fundraising page live and linked to the charity, live total or goal bar on screen, test donation run, alerts reading donor names only with permission and filtering offensive names or messages.
2. On-screen and panel text: what the stream is for, where the money goes, and a link to the official page. Tell viewers to donate only through that link.
3. Scam protection: mods remove any other donation links, the streamer never asks for money by direct message, and a pinned note says so.
4. Moderation brief for a high-traffic day: filters, slow mode, a plan for hate raids, and how mods reach the streamer.
5. Wellbeing plan: water, food, breaks, a person who can call a stop, and an agreed rule that the stream ends early rather than the streamer pushing through illness or exhaustion.
6. Tech pre-flight: backup scene, audio check, upload stability over a long run, and what happens if the stream drops.
