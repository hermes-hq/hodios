# Step 2: Choose the production model

1. Compare print-on-demand (no stock risk, lower margin, less control over quality), bulk stock from a manufacturer (better margin and quality, cash and storage risk, unsold stock), preorder-then-produce (no stock risk, long wait for buyers), and a merch partner or agency (less work, smaller share).
2. For this creator: the recommended model, given demand evidence, budget at risk, storage and time.
3. Supplier checklist: sample before ordering, print quality and sizes, production and shipping times, minimum order quantities, returns and misprint policy, where they ship from and duties to buyers' countries.
4. A short message to request quotes and samples.

Sections: Models compared, Recommendation, Supplier checklist, Quote request, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
