# Step 1: Test demand

1. Who would buy: the share of the audience that is engaged enough to pay, where they live (shipping cost and time), and the in-jokes or symbols that mean something to them.
2. Cheap tests before spending: a poll with mock-ups, a waitlist or interest form with an email field, a small preorder, or a single item on print-on-demand. Pick one or two and say what result would justify going ahead (for example sign-ups as a share of engaged viewers), stated as the creator's own threshold, not a market benchmark.
3. A short post or script to run the test.
4. Decision: go, adjust (different item or design), or stop.

Sections: Likely buyers, Tests, Test post, Decision rule, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
