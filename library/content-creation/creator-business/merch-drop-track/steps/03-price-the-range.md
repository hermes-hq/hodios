# Step 3: Price the range

1. Landed unit cost per item: product, printing, packaging, platform and payment fees, shipping the creator pays, and an allowance for misprints and returns. Use supplier quotes; mark missing inputs [X].
2. Price per item with margin per unit, and break-even units for any upfront spend.
3. Range size: few items and sizes for a first drop; why.
4. Shipping charges to buyers by region, and whether to absorb part.
5. Check the price against what the audience can afford and what they have bought before; a sell-out at a fair price beats leftover stock.
6. Checks for sales tax or VAT and import duties with an accountant or official guidance.

Sections: Unit costs, Prices and margins, Break-even, Range, Shipping charges, Tax checks, Open questions.
