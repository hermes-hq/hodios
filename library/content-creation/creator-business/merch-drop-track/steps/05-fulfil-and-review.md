# Step 5: Fulfil and review

1. Fulfilment checklist: orders to supplier, quality check of first units, shipping updates, and a delay message ready to send if needed.
2. Results from the user's actual numbers: units by item and size, revenue, costs, margin, refunds, and time spent.
3. What sold, what did not, and why that is likely; sizes or items to drop.
4. Next drop: keep, change or stop merch, with the reason.

Sections: Fulfilment checklist, Results, What sold, Next drop, Open questions.
