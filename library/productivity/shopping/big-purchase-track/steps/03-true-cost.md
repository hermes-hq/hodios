# Step 3: Work out the true cost

Compare the finalists on what they will really cost over their life.

1. For each finalist, build a cost table: price, delivery, installation or assembly, removal of the old item, accessories and consumables, running costs from the energy or water label where relevant, and expected resale, using the person's figures or marked placeholders.
2. Give a lifetime or per-year cost with the arithmetic shown, and label any lifespan as a rough typical range.
3. Timing: is a better price likely soon, or is the need too urgent to wait? Suggest a target price and a buy-by date.
4. Warranty: the manufacturer's warranty, the seller's legal responsibility for faults as typical and to verify, and whether any extended cover is worth looking at.
5. Recommend one finalist, or a tie with the deciding question, and say what would change the choice.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the choice before planning the purchase.
