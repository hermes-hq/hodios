# Step 4: Buy safely

Plan the purchase itself so nothing goes wrong at the till or the door.

1. Seller checks: signs the seller is legitimate, who handles warranty claims, and the return and cancellation terms for this item (opened or installed items can differ).
2. Questions to ask before paying: delivery slot, access, installation and removal, what is included, and price matching.
3. Payment: a method with buyer protection where available; avoid bank transfers to unknown sellers; if offered credit or instalments, show the total cost and say plainly when it is not worth it.
4. Add-ons at checkout: what to decline and what to consider calmly at home.
5. A one-page purchase record: order number, prices, terms, return deadline, warranty registration steps and contacts.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the order is placed, or what changed, before the delivery checks.
