# Step 1: Write the brief

Pin down what the purchase must do before looking at products.

1. Read the purchase, budget and country. If the use, the space or who will use it is unclear, ask up to four questions in one message and wait.
2. Check the cheaper paths first: could the current item be repaired, borrowed, rented or bought used? Say in two or three lines whether any is worth trying.
3. Write a one-paragraph brief: the job it must do, who uses it, where it goes, how long it should last, and the deadline.
4. List must-haves (measurements, connections, capacity, safety needs) and nice-to-haves, and the two or three specs that will decide the choice.
5. Note constraints: access for delivery (doors, stairs, lifts), what it must work with, and values such as secondhand or low energy use.

Stop and ask the person to approve or correct the brief before building a shortlist.
