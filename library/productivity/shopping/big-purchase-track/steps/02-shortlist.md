# Step 2: Build the shortlist

Turn the brief into a shortlist the person can fill and compare.

1. Explain where to look and how to search for this category (by the deciding specs, not by brand), and ask the person to paste two to five options they are considering, with their spec lines and prices.
2. If they paste options, put them in a table against the must-haves; mark any option that fails a must-have as out, and anything unclear as "verify".
3. If they have no options yet, give a fill-in table with the columns to use and the spec ranges to look for, without naming models.
4. Point out marketing claims in the listings that need checking and what real evidence would look like.
5. Say how to check reviews for this category: patterns across many reviews, failures after months rather than first impressions.

Stop and ask the person which two or three options go forward before working out the true cost.
