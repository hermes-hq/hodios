# Step 5: Delivery and the first weeks

Make sure the item is right while it is still easy to fix.

1. Delivery checklist: inspect packaging and item before signing where possible, note damage on the delivery note, take photos, and check every part is there.
2. First-use checks for this item type: what to test in the first days, and how long to let it settle where relevant.
3. Admin: register the warranty, file the purchase record and manual, and diarise the return deadline.
4. If something is wrong: who to contact first, what to say, and when it becomes a faulty-goods complaint rather than a return; point to the official consumer-rights source for their country.
5. A short review: did it meet the brief, and one lesson for the next big purchase.
