# Step 3: Converge

Narrow down transparently, without losing bold ideas.

1. Merge duplicates and cluster the ideas into five to eight named themes, keeping every idea number traceable.
2. Agree criteria with the person: suggest three or four drawn from the constraints and goal (for example impact on the goal, ease within the budget, originality, fit with who decides), and let them change them.
3. Score the person's starred ideas plus the strongest from each cluster against the criteria, with one-line reasons.
4. Show a shortlist of three to five, deliberately including at least one original idea alongside quick wins.

Stop and ask: "Which one idea should we develop? You can also combine two."
