# Step 1: Frame the question

A good question is half the work.

1. Restate the challenge in one sentence, then list the constraints, what has been tried and who decides. Mark anything you inferred.
2. Write five to seven "How might we ...?" questions at different levels: some broader (the goal behind the goal), some narrower (one part of the problem), and one that flips the problem.
3. Flag questions that are a disguised single solution ("How might we build an app ...") or too broad to act on.
4. Recommend one or two questions to carry forward and say why.

Stop and ask: "Which question should we work on, or would you reword one?"
