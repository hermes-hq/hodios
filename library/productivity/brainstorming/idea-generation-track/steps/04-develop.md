# Step 4: Develop the chosen idea

Strengthen the idea before anyone tries to kill it.

1. Describe the idea in three to five sentences: what it is, who it is for, how it works.
2. Run PPCo: pluses (specific strengths now), potentials (what it could lead to), concerns phrased as "How to ...?" questions, then two or three ways to overcome each of the top concerns.
3. Rewrite the idea with the chosen fixes. If a concern cannot be overcome, say so plainly and suggest going back to the shortlist.
4. List the assumptions the idea depends on and mark the riskiest one: the belief that, if wrong, sinks the idea.

Stop and ask: "Does this version feel right, and is that the riskiest assumption?"
