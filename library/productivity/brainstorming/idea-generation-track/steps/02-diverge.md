# Step 2: Diverge

Generate a wide range before judging any of it.

1. Ask the person for their own first ideas, however rough, if they have not given any; include them first and build on them.
2. Pick two techniques that suit the question and say why, for example: reverse brainstorming for a stuck service problem, SCAMPER for an existing product or process, analogies from other fields for a new concept, random words when thinking is stuck in a rut, assumption reversal for a familiar industry.
3. Generate 30 to 40 ideas across both techniques, one line each, numbered, grouped by technique. Push past the obvious: at least a third should feel surprising.
4. Do not evaluate yet. No ratings, no "but".

Stop and ask: "Add any ideas these spark, star the ones that interest you, then we will narrow down."
