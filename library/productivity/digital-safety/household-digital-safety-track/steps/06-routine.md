# Step 6: Routine

Keep it working with little effort.

1. Monthly (five minutes): check updates have installed and backups ran.
2. Every three months: review signed-in devices, shared users on cameras and family groups, and location sharing.
3. Every year: router firmware and passwords, children's limits as they grow, and the "if something goes wrong" sheet.
4. Pick a date and a person responsible for each, and add calendar reminders.

Finish with a short summary of what was done and what is left on the list.
