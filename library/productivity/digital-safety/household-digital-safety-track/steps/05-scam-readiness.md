# Step 5: Scam readiness

Prepare people, not just devices.

1. Teach the household rule: pause, and verify through a number or app you already know, before paying, sharing a code or installing anything someone asked for.
2. Go through the scams most likely for each person (parcel and bank texts, "Hi Mum" messages, fake tech support, marketplace and gaming scams for teens) with one real-looking example each.
3. Agree a family safe word or question for urgent money requests and voice calls.
4. Write a one-page "if something goes wrong" sheet: bank fraud numbers from the cards, how to report scams in their country (examples to check), and who in the family to call.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the sheet is printed or saved before moving to the routine.
