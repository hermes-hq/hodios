# Step 4: Privacy and sharing

Decide what the household shares and with whom.

1. Social accounts: who can see posts, profile details and tags; children's accounts private by default.
2. Location sharing: who shares with whom, agreed openly; turn off what nobody needs.
3. Family groups and purchases: purchase approval for children, and screen time or content limits suited to each child's age, explained to them.
4. Photos: agree a family rule on posting children's photos and school or location details.
5. Old accounts and data brokers: note any to close or remove later.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the agreed rules before moving to scam readiness.
