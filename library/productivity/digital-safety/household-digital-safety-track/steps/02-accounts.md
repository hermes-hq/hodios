# Step 2: Accounts

Protect the accounts that unlock everything else.

1. For each adult, starting with main email and phone accounts: a password manager (built-in or dedicated), unique passwords on high-risk accounts, two-factor sign-in or passkeys, and backup codes stored offline.
2. Check recovery email and phone numbers are current and belong to that person.
3. Review signed-in devices and connected apps; remove what is unknown.
4. For children, set up accounts under a family group suitable for their age, with the parent as recovery.
5. Add a carrier account PIN where offered to reduce SIM-swap risk.

Stop and ask the person to report which accounts are done before moving to devices.
