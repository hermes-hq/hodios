# Step 1: Inventory and priorities

Know what you have before changing anything.

1. With the person, list per household member: main email, phone account (Apple or Google), banking and payment apps, social accounts, and devices they use.
2. List shared things: the router, smart TV and cameras, streaming and shopping accounts, family groups, shared computers.
3. Mark the highest-risk items: main email accounts (they reset everything else), money accounts, and anyone who is more exposed (a child with a new phone, an older relative targeted by scammers).
4. Agree the top five fixes for this household, written as a short list.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the inventory and top five before moving to accounts.
