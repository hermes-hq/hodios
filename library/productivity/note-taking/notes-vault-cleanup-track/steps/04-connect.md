# Step 4: Connect

Make the cleaned collection easier to find your way around.

1. Ask for the titles, and a line each, of the notes in the two or three most-used areas.
2. Propose a hub note (map of content) for each area: sections, notes in order with exact titles as links, and a few words on each.
3. Suggest the ten strongest links between notes, with the relation (supports, contradicts, example of) and the sentence to add.
4. List gaps: notes the hubs suggest are missing.

Stop and ask the person to approve the hubs before setting up the routine.
