# Step 3: Prune and merge

Reduce clutter safely.

1. Remind the person to make a full backup or export first, and wait for a yes.
2. Give an action table for the step 1 candidates: keep, rename, move, merge into (named note), archive, or delete-after-check.
3. For each merge, list the notes, what to keep from each, and conflicts the person must resolve.
4. Order the work into 30-minute sessions, biggest gain first.

Stop and ask the person to approve the actions, or report back after the first session.
