# Step 1: Inventory

See what is there before changing anything.

1. If the inventory is not a list of notes, ask for one and stop.
2. Summarise: rough counts by folder or type, age spread, tag count, and the two or three biggest problems.
3. Group candidates, quoting titles: duplicates or overlaps, empty or "Untitled" notes, stale drafts, orphans, notes from a finished chapter of life, sensitive records to leave alone.
4. Say which checks you could not do because dates, sizes or links were missing.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the problems and the purpose before designing the structure.
