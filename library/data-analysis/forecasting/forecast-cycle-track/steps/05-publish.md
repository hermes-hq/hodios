# Step 5: Publish

1. Write the forecast summary for the users: the agreed forecast with its range, the change from last cycle and why (a bridge), the main risks and opportunities with rough sizes, and the decisions needed.
2. Recommend the chart (actuals plus a band showing the range) and plain wording for the uncertainty, such as "about 1 month in 5 will fall outside this range".
3. List what to watch before the next cycle and the triggers for an early re-forecast.
4. Carry forward open questions, owners and the adjustment log for scoring next cycle.

Sections: Forecast summary, Change from last cycle, Risks and opportunities, Chart and wording, What to watch, Carried forward.
