# Step 2: Staff training

1. Training needs by role from the approved journey: what each role must know, do and say.
2. Scenario-based sessions using real cases, including two awkward ones per role (an angry customer, a system down, an exception to the rule).
3. Timing close to launch, covering every shift and absent staff, with a short quick-reference card per role.
4. A sign-off: each person shows they can handle the core scenarios, not just attendance.

Sections: Needs by role (table), Sessions, Scenarios, Quick-reference cards, Sign-off, Open questions. Stop and wait for approval.
