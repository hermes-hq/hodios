# Step 5: Monitor and keep it down

1. Measures: return rate by sale cohort per SKU and channel, share by class, cost of returns per unit sold, and the return rate of changed SKUs against a baseline from step 1.
2. Timing: allow for the return window; judge a fix only on cohorts sold at least one full window after it went live.
3. Alerts: a SKU whose return rate rises by about a quarter on its own trailing average, any new defect pattern in a batch, and any safety-related reason (escalate at once).
4. Routine: a monthly 30-minute review of the top SKUs and classes, a quarterly check of reason codes and listings, and feeding new causes back into step 3.
5. Close the loop: tell support, listing and design owners what changed and what the data showed.
