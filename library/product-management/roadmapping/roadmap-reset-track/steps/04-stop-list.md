# Step 4: Decide what stops or waits

1. For every item below the line, give options: stop, delay to a named later period, shrink scope, or hand to another team. State the consequence of each (customer, revenue, trust, team).
2. Record the owner's decision per item and who must agree (requester, executive sponsor, customer owner). Do not record a decision the owner has not made.
3. Write the explicit "we will not do" list for the period.
4. Note follow-ups: contractual items for legal review, items needing an executive decision, re-entry triggers for delayed work.

Sections: Options per item (table), Decisions and approvers, Will not do, Follow-ups. Stop and wait for approval.
