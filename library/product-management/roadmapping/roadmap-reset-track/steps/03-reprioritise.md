# Step 3: Re-prioritise against outcomes

1. Confirm two to four outcomes from the goals; if none were given, ask for them and stop.
2. Put mandatory work first (contractual with remedies, legal, security, safety), labelled as such.
3. Rank the rest by contribution to an outcome, evidence, size and cost of delay. Show the reasoning per item in one line.
4. Draw the capacity line: what fits above it with a buffer of 10-20% for the unexpected.

Sections: Outcomes, Mandatory work, Ranked list with capacity line (table), Reasoning, Open questions. Stop and wait for approval.
