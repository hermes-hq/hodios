# Step 1: Inventory every commitment

1. List every piece of work promised from the roadmap and anywhere else mentioned: customer promises, executive requests, partner deals, compliance and security work, maintenance and in-flight work.
2. For each: id, item, who asked, source (roadmap, contract, email, meeting, ticket), strength (contractual, written promise, verbal, internal wish), date promised, owner, status, rough size.
3. Ask the owner where else commitments might be hiding (sales, account managers, leadership, support escalations) and add what they report.
4. Mark duplicates and items that are really the same need.

Sections: Commitment inventory (table), Hidden commitments found, Duplicates, Open questions. Stop and wait for approval.
