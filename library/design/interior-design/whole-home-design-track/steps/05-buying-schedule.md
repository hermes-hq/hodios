# Step 5: Buying and install schedule

Turn the approved phases into dated actions.

1. Order the tasks by dependency: trades and decorating before flooring where needed, flooring before rugs and furniture, measuring before ordering, long lead-time items ordered early.
2. Give a dated schedule per phase with who does each task (household, delivery, qualified trade).
3. Add a buying checklist for each large item: measurements, route-in check, return policy, delivery date, assembly and wall anchoring.
4. End with a short "home rules" card that repeats the direction and purchase rules from step 2, for future buying decisions.
