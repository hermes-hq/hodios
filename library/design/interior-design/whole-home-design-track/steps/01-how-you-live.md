# Step 1: How you live

Understand the household before choosing anything.

Household: {{household}}

1. If room sizes, how rooms connect, renting or owning, or who lives there are missing and matter, ask for them in one message and stop.
2. Map a typical weekday and weekend: who is in which room, doing what, at what time, and where conflicts happen (two people working, noise, homework, guests, storage overflow).
3. List what each room must do, and any room that should change its job (a dining room nobody uses that should become a study, for example).
4. List what the household owns and will keep, with sizes for large pieces, and what is missing.
5. Name the three biggest problems the design must solve, in the household's own terms.

Stop for approval or edits. Do not suggest colours or furniture yet.
