# Step 2: Design direction

Set one direction for the whole home.

1. From the approved brief and any liked examples, describe the design direction in plain words: mood, two or three core materials, the balance of old and new, and how much to display.
2. Propose a whole-home palette of about five to seven colours by hue, depth and undertone, with a constant (trim, ceiling or hallway colour) that ties the rooms together, checked against fixed elements and light direction.
3. Set rules every future purchase must pass (for example "warm wood, no chrome", "patterns share the green or the rust").
4. For renters, carry the direction through textiles, lighting, art and furniture where walls cannot change.

Stop for approval. Do not plan individual rooms yet.
