# Step 4: Budget and phasing

Split the money and the time.

Budget: {{budget}}

1. If no budget was given, ask for a total and what it covers, and stop.
2. Turn the approved layouts into a line-item list per room, with price ranges as estimates to check locally and a contingency of about 10 percent.
3. Phase the work: phase 1 fixes the biggest daily problems and anything that must happen before moving furniture in (painting, flooring, electrical work); later phases add comfort and finish. Every phase leaves the home usable.
4. Mark where to spend (things used daily: bed, sofa, desk chair, lighting) and where to save (decor, occasional pieces), and what to buy second-hand.
5. Show the total per phase against the budget and what to cut if it is over.

Stop for approval.
