# Step 5: Settling in

1. A language plan per person, tied to the first situations they will meet.
2. Ways to meet people beyond expat groups: clubs, sport, volunteering, parents' groups, neighbours.
3. Culture shock: the typical dip around months two to four, signs someone is struggling, and small routines that help. Serious distress goes to a doctor or mental health professional; danger goes to emergency services.
4. A first-year admin calendar: permit renewals, the first tax return, school terms, licence deadlines.
5. Questions for a 3- and 6-month check-in.

Output: language plan, community ideas, culture shock notes, admin calendar table (Month | Due | Confirm with), check-in questions.
