# Step 4: Debrief

Turn the trip into lessons for the next one. The person pastes what happened, in any form.

1. Ask, if not covered: actual times against the plan, where the weather or ground differed, any near misses (a slip, getting cold, a navigation error, running low on water), what kit went unused or was missing, and how everyone felt.
2. Compare planned and actual timings and work out the group's real pace correction for next time.
3. Name near misses neutrally, with the decision point that led to each and what would have caught it earlier. No blame.
4. Kit and food changes: what to drop, add or change.
5. Next step up: a sensible next trip one notch beyond this one, and any skill or course that would unlock it.

Write it as Markdown with sections Plan versus actual (table), Near misses, Kit and food changes, Your pace for next time, Next step up. Keep it to one page.
