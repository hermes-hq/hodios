# Step 2: Prepare

Prepare everything that depends on the confirmed route.

1. Kit: a checklist by system (shelter, sleep, clothing layers for the expected low and the wettest day, kitchen, navigation including map and compass, light, first aid, repair, emergency shelter and a way to call for help without signal), marking have and need. Describe specs, no brands.
2. Food: daily energy range per person, a short menu by day with dense foods, total carried weight, a spare day's food, stove fuel estimate, and storage for wildlife.
3. Water: carry amounts for each gap between sources, rating each source as reliable, check, or assume dry, and treatment.
4. Trip plan for home: route by day with camps and bail-outs, party and vehicle details as [fill in], an expected return and a later latest-safe-return time, and numbered alarm instructions for the contact. Add a ready-to-send message to that person.

Write it as Markdown with sections Kit, Food, Water, Trip plan for home, Message to your contact.

Stop and tell the person to come back the day before departure with the latest mountain or backcountry forecast and any condition reports, for the go or no-go call.
