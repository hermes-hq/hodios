# Step 5: Debrief after the real appointment

1. Ask the learner what happened: what they got, what they did not understand, any words they heard and noted, and how they felt.
2. Check each "Must get" item: secured, partly or not. For anything not secured, draft a short follow-up message or call script in {{target_language}}.
3. Explain the words or phrases they noted that confused them, with meanings.
4. Learn next: the 5 phrases from this appointment to keep for future visits, and the one skill to practise next (for example numbers, phone calls, saying no).
5. If what they report involves a decision on health, law, immigration or money, say who to ask; do not interpret it for them.

Sections: What happened, Must get outcome, Follow-up, Words explained, Learn next.
