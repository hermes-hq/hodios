# Step 4: Sample lesson

Test the design by writing one complete lesson from a unit the user chooses (default: unit 2).

1. Aim as a can-do outcome linked to a course goal, and the language focus.
2. Stage plan with timings that fit one session: warm-up recycling earlier language, input in context, clarification, controlled practice, a communicative task linked to the unit task, feedback and homework. Learners talk for most of the lesson.
3. Materials in full, in {{target_language}} at the right level, with answers.
4. Differentiation for the level range in the group.
5. What the lesson revealed about the syllabus (too much in the unit, a missing prerequisite) and any change to propose.

Sections: Aim, Lesson plan, Materials, Differentiation, Syllabus feedback, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
