# Step 1: Pillars

Premise:
{{premise}}
{{#medium}}Medium: {{medium}}{{/medium}}

1. If the premise gives no genre or central conflict, ask for both in one message and stop.
2. List what the author has already fixed, and anything that seems to conflict.
3. Propose three to five pillars: short statements of what makes this world distinct and what every later choice must support (for example "memory is currency", "the sea is rising and everyone knows it"). For each, say how it serves the central conflict.
4. State the tone, the scale (one city, a region, a continent, a star system) and the technology or magic level in one line each.
5. List what the setting does not need yet, so later steps stay focused.

Stop and wait for the author to approve or change the pillars.
