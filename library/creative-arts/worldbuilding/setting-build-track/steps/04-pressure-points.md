# Step 4: Pressure points

Using everything approved so far:

1. Identify five to seven pressure points where the world is about to change or break (a dwindling resource, a disputed succession, a new technology, a broken treaty), each linked to factions and pillars.
2. Connect the central conflict of the premise to at least two of them, and show how the protagonists or player characters are caught up.
3. Run a quick logic check: the most obvious way a clever character could solve the main conflict using the world's rules, and why it does not work or what it costs.
4. List open questions only the author should decide.

Stop and wait for the author to approve or change this step.
