# Step 3: Peoples and power

Using the approved pillars, land and history:

1. Describe two to four peoples or cultures: how they live, what they value, what they believe, and their internal disagreements. Root each trait in the land and history, and avoid thin disguises of real-world groups.
2. Describe who holds power: governments, faiths, guilds, companies or other institutions, what each wants, and the coalition each depends on.
3. If the setting has magic or unusual technology, give its key rules, costs and who controls access in a few lines.
4. Give a table of three to six factions: Faction | Wants | Resources | Rival.
5. Check: each faction's goal clashes with at least one other; every people has people within it who disagree.

Stop and wait for the author to approve or change this step.
