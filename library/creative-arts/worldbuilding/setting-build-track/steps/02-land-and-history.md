# Step 2: Land and history

Using the approved pillars:

1. Describe the land at the chosen scale: four to six regions with terrain, climate, resources and why people live there, plus how travel works and roughly how long key journeys take.
2. Build the history as three to five eras, each with a cause, a turning point and a trace visible in the story's present (a ruin, a border, a grudge, a festival).
3. Name one event the different peoples remember differently, and how each tells it.
4. Check: geography and history support every pillar; nothing contradicts what the author fixed.
