# Step 2: Outline

1. Choose a structure (chronological, problem then solution, framework, argument, thematic) and say why it beats the next-best option.
2. Chapter map: working title, job in the book, key claim, evidence or story needed, bridge to the next chapter.
3. Check it: merge chapters that make the same point, flag thin ones, and confirm the first chapter earns trust and the last pays off the promise.
4. Estimate total and per-chapter length (trade nonfiction often runs 60,000 to 90,000 words) and pick the first chapter to draft as the voice test.

Sections: Structure, Chapter map (table), Overlap check, Length, First chapter. Stop and wait for approval.
