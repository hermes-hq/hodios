# Step 1: Concept

Turn the idea for "{{working_title}}" into a concept strong enough to structure.

1. Ask, in one message, only what you cannot infer: the idea if none was given, genre and tone, the intended audience and budget level (micro, indie, studio), and anything that must stay (a character, a scene, an ending).
2. Once answered, write three concept options. Each has a logline (protagonist with a flaw, inciting incident, goal, opposition, stakes), the central dramatic question, the hook that would make someone read it, and the closest genre expectations it must meet.
3. For each option, name its biggest risk (passive protagonist, small stakes, familiar premise, budget beyond the level) in one line.
4. Recommend one option, or a merge, and say why.

Write the document with sections Answers, Options, Recommendation.

Stop and wait for the writer to choose or adjust the concept.
