# Step 3: Scene outline

Expand the approved beat sheet into a scene-by-scene outline the draft can follow.

1. List every scene in order with a slugline (INT. or EXT., place, time), who is in it, what the protagonist or point-of-view character wants in it, the turn, and an estimated page length.
2. Mark the act breaks and the beats from step 2 on the scenes that deliver them.
3. Flag scenes with no turn, two scenes doing the same job, and any stretch over ten pages where the protagonist's position does not change; propose a fix for each.
4. Sum the page estimates and compare them with the target length.

Write the document with sections Scene outline, Flags, Length check.

Stop and wait for the writer to approve the outline.
