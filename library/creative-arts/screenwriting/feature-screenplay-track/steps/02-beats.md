# Step 2: Beat sheet

Build the structure for the approved concept.

1. Write the story spine: protagonist's external goal, internal need, antagonist, stakes, theme as a question, and the ending.
2. Build a beat sheet in a three-act or Save the Cat shape scaled to the target length, with page targets. For each beat, give the specific scene that delivers it and what changes, linked by cause ("therefore" or "but", never "and then").
3. Describe the A and B stories and where they intersect.
4. Check, marking OK or Flag: the protagonist makes the key choices at the act-one break, the midpoint and the climax; the midpoint changes the game; the all-is-lost moment costs something real; the climax is won by what the protagonist learned.

Write the document with sections Spine, Beat sheet, Storylines, Checks.

Stop and wait for the writer to approve or change the beats.
