# Step 4: First ten pages

Draft the opening to test voice, tone and how quickly the story grips.

1. Write the first ten or so pages in standard screenplay format, following the approved outline: sluglines, lean action lines of one to three lines, character introductions in caps with an age and a telling action, and dialogue with subtext. No camera directions.
2. By page ten the reader should know the protagonist, their want or flaw, the world, the tone and the genre promise, and feel the disruption coming.
3. After the pages, add a short self-check: what the reader knows by page ten, the line or moment most likely to lose a reader, and two choices the writer might want to make differently.

Write the document with sections Pages, Self-check.

Stop and wait for the writer's reaction before planning the draft.
