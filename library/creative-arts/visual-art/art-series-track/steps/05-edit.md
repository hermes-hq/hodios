# Step 5: Edit and sequence

Choose and order the final series from what was made.

1. Ask for the full set (images or a list with notes) if not given.
2. Sort pieces into keep, maybe and cut, with a reason for each. Cut pieces that repeat a stronger one or break the rules without adding anything.
3. Sequence the keepers for a wall, a portfolio or a book: an opening piece, a rhythm of scale and intensity, and a closing piece.
4. Name any gap (a missing size, a needed quiet piece) and whether to make one more.

Stop and wait for the artist to confirm the edit.
