# Step 1: Concept

Find what the series is about.

Starting point: {{idea}}
Time and output: {{timeframe}}

1. If the starting point is only a medium or a vague wish ("something with colour"), ask up to three questions about subjects, places or questions the artist keeps returning to, and stop.
2. Write three possible series concepts, each as one sentence on what the work is about plus one sentence on how it will look. Make them genuinely different: one close to existing work, one stretch, one bold.
3. For each concept, name the subject pool (where the artist will find material), the risk (too broad, too thin, already done often) and what would make it theirs.
4. Recommend one and say why.

Stop and wait for the artist to choose or reshape a concept.
