# Step 6: Present the series

Prepare the series to show, sell or submit.

1. Write a series title and two short versions of a series text: 50 words and 150 words, in plain first-person language, using only what the artist has said about the work.
2. Draft individual titles following the titling rule.
3. List next steps that fit the output in {{timeframe}}: photographing, framing, pricing as a set, open calls, gallery approaches, a solo show or a book.
4. Write a short note on what the artist learned and an idea seed for the next series.
