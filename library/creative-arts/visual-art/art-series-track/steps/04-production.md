# Step 4: Production plan

Plan making the full series to {{timeframe}}.

1. Set the target number of finished pieces plus a 30 to 50 percent surplus, since some will be cut.
2. Write a schedule by week or month: pieces in progress, drying or curing time, photography of finished work, and buffer.
3. Add materials and a rough budget method: surfaces in the chosen sizes, paint or ink quantities, framing if needed later.
4. Set a midpoint review: lay out or pin up everything, check against the rules, and decide what to drop or add.

Stop and wait for the artist to approve the plan. They will come back after making the work.
