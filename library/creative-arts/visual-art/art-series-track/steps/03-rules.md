# Step 3: Series rules

Turn the best studies into rules that make the pieces belong together.

1. From the artist's report, name the two or three studies that worked and why.
2. Write the series rules: format and sizes, medium and surface, palette, subject range, composition habits, level of finish, titling scheme. Five to eight rules, each specific enough to check.
3. Name what is allowed to vary, so the series does not become repetitive.
4. Plan two test pieces at full size that follow the rules, and what would mean a rule needs changing.

Stop and wait for the artist to make the test pieces and approve or adjust the rules.
