# Step 2: Studies and research

Explore the chosen concept before committing.

1. Plan six to ten small studies (an hour or less each) that test the concept in different ways: subject choices, crops, palettes, scale, mark-making, materials.
2. List research to gather: own reference photos or sketches on site, objects, reading, and two or three artists who have worked near this theme, with what to look at in each (not to copy).
3. Say what to look for when reviewing the studies: which ones carry the idea, which look like the artist, which they enjoyed making.

Stop and wait for the artist to make the studies and report back with images or notes on what worked.
