# Step 3: Order

1. Propose an opening poem that teaches the reader how to read the book, and a closing poem that leaves the right feeling; give one alternative for each.
2. Propose an order with a one-line reason for each placement, varying length and tone and placing linked poems so they speak to each other without repeating.
3. Decide on sections: none, or two or three with a reason. Many chapbooks use none.
4. Offer three to five title options, from a poem title, a phrase in the book, or a phrase that names the core.
5. Read the order as a whole and note any dip in energy.

Write the document with sections Opening and closing, Order, Sections, Title options, Read-through.

Stop and wait for the poet to approve the order and title.
