# Step 4: Finish the manuscript

1. List the front and back matter: title page, contents, acknowledgments of prior publication (only the credits the poet gives), notes on sources or epigraphs, and a short bio if the route asks for one. Draft the acknowledgments and notes from the poet's information, with [check] where details are missing.
2. Give a formatting checklist common to many guidelines: each poem starting on a new page, a readable 11 or 12 point font, page numbers, consistent titles, and for blind-judged contests no name anywhere in the file. Say to check the specific guidelines.
3. Check the page count against the usual chapbook range and the route; say what to cut or add if it is outside.
4. Do a final consistency pass: title capitalisation, repeated words across adjacent poems, epigraph credits.

Write the document with sections Front and back matter, Formatting checklist, Page count, Consistency notes.

Stop and wait for the poet to confirm the manuscript is finished.
