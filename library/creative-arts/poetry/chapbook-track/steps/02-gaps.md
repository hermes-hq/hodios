# Step 2: Gaps and revision triage

1. Read the chosen poems as one sequence and name what is missing: a part of the story or argument not covered, a missing register (no quiet poem, no funny one), too many poems of the same length or shape.
2. Suggest two to five poems the poet could write to fill gaps, each as a one-line prompt tied to the core, not a finished poem.
3. Triage the existing poems: ready, needs a light revision (say what in one line), needs a deep revision, or cut. Keep the poet's strongest poems even if they need work.
4. Flag near-duplicates (two poems doing the same job) and suggest which to keep.
5. Give a realistic work list with an order and, if there is a deadline, rough dates.

Write the document with sections What is missing, Poems to write, Triage, Work list.

Stop and wait for the poet to finish or approve the work list and send the updated poems.
