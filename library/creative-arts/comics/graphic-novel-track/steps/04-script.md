# Step 4: Script chapter one

Write chapter one in full-script form from the approved page breakdown.

1. Use a PAGE heading with number, side and panel count, PANEL headings with a present-tense description for the artist (shot size, angle, who is where, action, expression, key props), and numbered lettering beneath (CAPTION, CHARACTER, SFX).
2. Keep one moment per panel, and keep dialogue to the lines that move the scene; let the art carry what it can show.
3. After the script, count panels and lettered words per page and flag any page over the budget.
4. Add notes for the artist (recurring motifs, key acting moments, character consistency) and list any invented visual details as questions.

Write the document with sections Script, Load counts, Notes for the artist, Questions.

Stop and wait for approval or revisions before the production plan.
