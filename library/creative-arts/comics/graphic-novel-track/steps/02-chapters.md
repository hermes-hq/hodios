# Step 2: Chapter breakdown

Divide the approved story into chapters that each work as a unit.

1. Break the story into chapters (or volumes, then chapters) with a page budget for each that adds up to the approved total.
2. For each chapter give what happens, the main character's state at the start and end, the key image or set piece, and the hook or turn that leads into the next chapter.
3. Mark the story's major beats (inciting incident, midpoint, crisis, climax) by page number and check that the climax gets enough pages.
4. Flag chapters that carry too much for their page budget, and subplots that never pay off.

Write the document with sections Chapter table (Chapter | Pages | What happens | Key image | Hook), Story beats by page, Flags.

Stop and wait for approval before breaking chapter one into pages.
