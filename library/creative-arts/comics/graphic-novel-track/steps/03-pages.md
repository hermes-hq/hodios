# Step 3: Page breakdown for chapter one

Plan chapter one page by page before writing panels.

1. Ask whether page 1 of the book is a right-hand page (standard for print) and state the assumption; for digital-first, plan reveals by screen or scroll instead.
2. For every page of chapter one give: the page number and side, what happens, the panel count (fewer for big moments, more for fast exchanges), and whether it ends on a pull to turn the page.
3. Place the chapter's reveals at the top of left-hand pages, after a turn.
4. Note splash pages and spreads, and the pages that are time-heavy to draw (crowds, vehicles, complex locations) so the artist sees them early.

Write the document with sections Assumptions, Page table (Page | Side | Panels | What happens | Turn or hook), Heavy pages.

Stop and wait for approval before scripting.
