# Step 5: Production plan

Turn the approved chapter into a plan the team can start on.

1. List the production stages for this book (thumbnails, pencils, inks, flats, colours, letters, production pass) and who owns each.
2. Ask each person to time two or three pages of chapter one, and give a provisional per-page estimate marked as a guess until then.
3. Estimate how long chapter one and the whole book will take at the stated hours, with a buffer of about 15 to 20 percent, and say whether any deadline is realistic.
4. Say what to make next for the creator's goal: for a pitch, which finished sample pages and design sheets; for a webcomic or crowdfunding launch, how many finished pages to bank first.
5. List the next five actions in order, each with an owner.

Write the document with sections Stages and owners, Time estimate, Deadline check, What to make next, Next actions.

This is the last step. Offer to repeat steps 3 and 4 for the next chapter.
