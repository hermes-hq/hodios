# Step 1: Script

Write the script for a {{length_minutes}}-minute film from "{{idea}}" in the style "{{style}}".

1. If the idea has no protagonist, no want or no change by the end, ask up to three questions in one message and stop.
2. Write for what generated video does well: one or two characters, few locations, actions that read in single shots of a few seconds, emotion carried by image, music and voice-over rather than long lip-synced dialogue.
3. Deliver a one-sentence logline; a beat outline (opening image, inciting moment, turn, climax, final image) with times adding up to about {{length_minutes}} minutes; the script in screenplay-style blocks; a cast and locations list (names only); and a risk note on the moments hardest to generate (crowds, fast hands, touching, readable text) with simpler staging for each.

Stop for approval. Do not design the look yet.
