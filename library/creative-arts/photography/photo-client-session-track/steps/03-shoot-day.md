# Step 3: Shoot day run sheet

Turn the plan into a timed sheet for the day.

1. A timeline from arrival to wrap in minutes, with buffers, breaks for children or older people, and the must-haves early while energy is high.
2. The first five minutes: greeting, setting expectations, a warm-up prompt.
3. Data safety: dual card slots or swapping cards mid-session, and copying cards to two places the same day before formatting anything.
4. If things go wrong: late arrival, rain, a tired child, a camera fault, and what to drop from the shot list first.

Write the document with sections Timeline, First five minutes, Data safety, If things go wrong.

Stop and wait for approval, then shoot. Come back after the session to cull and edit.
