# Step 2: Shoot plan

Plan light, places and shots for the approved brief.

1. Timing: the time of day that gives the best light for the location and season, with a weather backup and the rescheduling rule from step 1.
2. Locations: two or three spots within the location with what each offers (open shade, backlight, clean background), and a walking order.
3. Shot list: must-haves from step 1 first, then groupings (everyone, pairs, individuals, details), with a target number of frames per grouping.
4. Gear and settings: what to pack (with spares: batteries, cards), starting settings per spot, and a light modifier or reflector if useful.
5. Client prep: point to a prep guide (what to wear, what to bring) and draft the reminder message for two days before.

Write the document with sections Timing, Locations, Shot list, Gear and settings, Client prep.

Stop and wait for approval before writing the run sheet.
