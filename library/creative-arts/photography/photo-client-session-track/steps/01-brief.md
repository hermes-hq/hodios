# Step 1: Brief

Pin down what the client needs before planning anything.

1. From the request, write what is known: session type, people, date, location ideas, use of the photos (wall prints, cards, website), deadline.
2. List what is missing and write a short, friendly reply to the client asking only those questions (who will be there, must-have shots, style they like, any mobility, sensory or cultural needs, consent for sharing images of children or on the photographer's portfolio).
3. Note the terms that must be agreed in writing before the date (session length, images included, delivery time, rescheduling, deposit, usage), using the photographer's own terms or placeholders.
4. Flag risks: tight deadline, large group, midday-only availability, indoor venue with poor light.

Write the document with sections Known, Questions for the client, Terms to confirm, Risks.

Stop and wait for approval before planning the shoot.
