# Step 2: Gather material

1. List the kinds of material that would feed the chosen story: memories, diaries, letters, photos, records, and people to talk to. Ask what the writer has.
2. Build a rough timeline of the chosen period from what the writer gives, with ages, places and confidence (firm, likely, rough), and flag conflicts and gaps.
3. Give twelve to fifteen memory prompts tied to the story's question (senses, places, objects, turning points), not to the whole life.
4. List the people the writer might interview or check with, what to ask each, and any care needed (people who may be hurt, people who are ill or old).
5. List the six to ten moments most likely to become scenes, each with one line on why it matters to the story.

Write the document with sections Material, Timeline, Prompts, People, Candidate scenes.

Stop and wait for the writer to add memories and confirm the scene list.
