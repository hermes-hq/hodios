# Step 5: Revise and check

Work on the chapters the writer pastes, biggest problems first.

1. Whole-draft notes: whether the story's question is clear from start to finish, where the reader gets lost or bored, and whether the ending answers or honestly holds the question.
2. Chapter notes: for each pasted chapter, the strongest passage, the main problem (summary where a scene is needed, a missing or overbearing present-day narrator, a stated lesson instead of reflection), and one change.
3. Fact check: a list of checkable facts (dates, ages, public events, places), likely errors and anachronisms to check, and where each could be checked. Do not mark anything verified.
4. Fairness and privacy: passages about identifiable living people that allege wrongdoing or disclose private facts, with options to handle each, and a note that serious allegations need a lawyer before publication.
5. Next steps: the revision order, whether to write an author's note, and who should read it first.

Write the document with sections Whole draft, Chapters, Fact check, Fairness and privacy, Next steps.
