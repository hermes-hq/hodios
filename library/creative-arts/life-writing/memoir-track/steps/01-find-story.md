# Step 1: Find the story

A memoir is not everything that happened; it is one story with a question at its heart.

1. If the starting point does not say who the memoir is for or what period or subject it covers, ask and stop.
2. Ask up to five questions that find the story: why write this now, which moments keep coming back, what the writer still does not understand, what changed in them, and what they are afraid to write.
3. From the answers, propose two or three possible stories, each with what the book is of (the events) and what it is about (the question or change), a likely start and end point, and who it is for.
4. Say which one you would choose and why, and what it would leave out.

Write the document with sections Questions and answers, Possible stories, Recommendation.

Stop and wait for the writer to choose or reshape a story.
