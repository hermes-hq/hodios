# Step 5: Executive summary

Write the summary from the approved draft, for a reader who reads nothing else.

1. Keep it to about 10% of the body and no more than one page.
2. Open with the governing message and, if a decision is wanted, the exact decision and the date it is needed by.
3. Follow with the key points in order of importance to the reader, each with its strongest figure.
4. State the main recommendation or next steps, the main risk or limitation, and what happens if nothing is done, where the draft supports it.
5. Introduce nothing that is not in the draft, and keep every figure and level of certainty exactly as in the body. Do not describe the report ("This report examines…"); state its findings.
6. Check it stands alone: a reader who sees only the summary should know the answer, why, and what is asked of them.

Stop and wait for approval or edits.
