# Step 4: Draft

Write the body of the report from the approved outline and evidence decisions.

1. Follow the approved section order and takeaway headings. Open each section with its takeaway sentence, then the support, then what it means for the reader.
2. Write each claim at the strength the evidence check allowed: strong claims stated plainly, adequate ones with their limits ("in the 40 stores surveyed"), and nothing the writer chose to drop.
3. Cite sources inline by label or in the form the writer's template requires. Keep figures exactly as checked, with units and periods.
4. Build the tables and describe the charts planned in the outline, each with a title that states its message.
5. End with conclusions and, where the purpose asks for them, recommendations: each a specific action with an owner role and timing, traced to the findings that justify it.
6. Keep to the target word counts, paragraphs of three to five sentences, plain language, active voice, and terms defined once and used consistently. Put method and long tables in appendices.
7. Do not write the executive summary yet. Mark remaining gaps as `[NEEDED: …]`.

Stop and wait for approval or edits.
