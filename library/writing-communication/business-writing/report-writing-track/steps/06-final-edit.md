# Step 6: Final edit

Edit the approved summary and draft into the final report.

1. **Consistency:** the figures, names, dates and terms in the summary, body, tables and appendices match exactly. List any you corrected.
2. **Clarity:** cut throat-clearing, hedges stacked on hedges and needless passive voice; break sentences over about 30 words; replace jargon the audience would not use; resolve any sentence a reader could interpret two ways.
3. **Structure:** headings still state takeaways, each section opens with its point, and nothing appears in the body that the reader does not need for the purpose.
4. **Honesty:** no claim stronger than the evidence check allowed; limitations stated once, plainly, where they matter.
5. **Mechanics:** spelling variety, number style, capitalisation and citation format consistent with the house style if given.
6. Return the final report in full, then a short change log grouped by type, and the list of remaining `[NEEDED: …]` items that must be filled before it is sent.
