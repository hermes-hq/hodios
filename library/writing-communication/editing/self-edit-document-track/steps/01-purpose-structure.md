# Step 1: Purpose and structure

1. If the reader or purpose is unclear, ask and stop.
2. State the document's main point in one or two sentences. If you cannot, that is the first problem: say what competing points you found.
3. Reverse-outline it: one line per section saying what it does for the reader.
4. Flag sections that are out of order, repeat each other, do not serve the purpose, or are missing (for example no recommendation, no next steps, background with no conclusion).
5. Check the opening: can the reader learn the main point and why it matters from the first paragraph or summary?
6. Propose a revised outline with moves, cuts, merges and additions, each with a reason, and estimate the length change against any limit.

Write sections Main point, Reverse outline, Problems, Proposed outline. Stop and wait for approval before touching paragraphs.
