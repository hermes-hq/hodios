# Step 3: Lines

1. Edit sentences for clarity and economy: cut filler and throat-clearing, prefer concrete verbs to nominalisations, break sentences that carry more than one main idea, put the important information at the end of the sentence.
2. Keep the writer's voice and the register the reader expects.
3. Show the edited text with changes marked, a section at a time if long, and name the two or three habits behind most edits so the writer can fix them next time.
4. Report the word count before and after, against any limit.

Stop for approval before the consistency pass.
