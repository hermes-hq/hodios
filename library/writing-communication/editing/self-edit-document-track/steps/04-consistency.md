# Step 4: Consistency

1. Build a short style sheet from the approved text: spelling variety, terms and their one chosen form, capitalisation, numbers and units, dates, abbreviations (defined at first use), list and heading style, and any style guide named in the brief.
2. List every inconsistency with its locations and the form you propose.
3. Check internal references: section numbers, "see above" and "as noted" pointers, figure and table mentions, and that totals and repeated figures match each other.
4. Apply only the approved fixes.

Stop for approval of the style sheet and fixes.
