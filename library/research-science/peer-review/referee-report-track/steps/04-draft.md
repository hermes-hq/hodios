# Step 4: Draft the report

From the approved major and minor list:

1. Write a short summary paragraph and an overall assessment naming real strengths and the main concerns.
2. Write each major issue as: location, problem, why it matters, what would resolve it. Request new data only where a conclusion cannot stand without it.
3. Write minor issues in one or two lines each.
4. Propose a recommendation that follows from the major issues, with the deciding reasons.
5. Draft confidential comments to the editor, or "None".

Write sections: Summary, Overall assessment, Major issues, Minor issues, Recommendation, Confidential comments to the editor. Stop and wait for approval.
