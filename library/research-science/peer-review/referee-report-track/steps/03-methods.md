# Step 3: Methods and evidence check

Using the approved summary and claims:

1. Check design against the question: sample and its justification, measures, controls, and analysis fit (clustering, repeated measures, model choice).
2. Check multiplicity, missing data, effect sizes with intervals, and consistency of numbers across abstract, text and tables; show any recalculation.
3. Check reproducibility: data, code, materials and enough method detail to repeat.
4. Check whether each central claim follows from the results; flag causal or general claims that outrun the design.
5. Sort findings into major (could change the conclusions or the decision) and minor.

Write sections: Design and analysis, Numbers checked, Reproducibility, Claims that do not follow, Major and minor list. Stop and wait for approval.
