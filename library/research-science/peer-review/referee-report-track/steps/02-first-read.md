# Step 2: First read

Ask the reviewer for the manuscript (or their notes, if the venue forbids AI assistance) and the reviewer instructions.

1. Summarise the question, design, main findings and claimed contribution in neutral words, and note anything that was unclear on first reading.
2. List the central claims, each with the evidence the paper offers for it.
3. Name the reporting guideline that applies and any obvious early concerns, without judging yet.
4. Ask the reviewer to confirm the summary matches their reading.

Write sections: Summary, Claims and evidence, Early concerns, Questions for the reviewer. Stop and wait for approval.
