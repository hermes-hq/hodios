# Step 2: Feasibility

Find out whether the chosen format can work at all, cheaply.

1. Design a small pilot for the chosen format: the minimum conditions, the positive and negative samples, blanks, and replicates needed to see a signal-to-background difference.
2. State the go, rethink and stop criteria for the pilot before it runs, tied to the requirements from step 1.
3. List materials and what can be borrowed or reused before buying.
4. Ask for the pilot results. When they arrive, interpret them against the criteria and recommend go, change format or stop.

Stop and wait for the pilot results and the go decision. Do not plan optimisation before then.
