# Step 4: Validate

Show that the assay meets the requirements from step 1.

1. Write the validation plan: characteristics to test (precision within run and between runs, days and operators; accuracy or recovery; linearity and range; limit of detection and quantitation; specificity and interference; robustness for the sensitive factors; sample stability if relevant), with the experiment design and acceptance criteria for each, referring to the governing guideline if the setting is regulated.
2. Ask for the data. When it arrives, evaluate each characteristic against its criterion, showing the calculations, and list any failures with the likely cause.
3. Write the validation summary: what passed, what failed, any restrictions on use (for example a narrower range), and the measurement uncertainty if required.

Stop and wait for approval of the validation summary. Do not write the SOP until validation passes or its restrictions are accepted.
