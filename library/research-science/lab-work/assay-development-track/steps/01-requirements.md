# Step 1: Requirements

Define what the assay must do before any bench work.

1. Write the intended use in one or two sentences: the analyte, the sample type, the decision the result supports, and who runs it.
2. List the performance requirements with target values or [CONFIRM] placeholders: measuring range, required sensitivity (limit of detection or quantitation), precision, accuracy or recovery, specificity against likely interferents, sample volume, throughput, time to result and cost per sample.
3. Say which requirements are must-have and which are nice-to-have, and which guideline governs validation if the setting is regulated.
4. List candidate assay formats with a one-line case for each, and the main risk of each.
5. List the questions the user must answer before feasibility work.

Stop and wait for approval of the requirements and the shortlisted formats.
