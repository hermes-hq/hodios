# Step 3: Checking

1. Check each claim against its source, highest risk first. Quotes against recordings or notes word for word; numbers against the original table with units and dates; descriptions of events against at least one independent source where possible.
2. Set a status per claim: Verified (source named), Verified with change (give the corrected wording), Disputed (sources disagree), Open (no source yet), or Cut recommended.
3. For people described in the text, note whether they have been given a chance to respond to significant allegations, and flag any that have not.
4. Update the claim log and list every proposed text change.

Write sections: Updated claim log, Proposed changes, Open and disputed claims, Legal review flags. Stop and wait for approval.
