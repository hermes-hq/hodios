# Step 1: Claim log

1. Go through the manuscript and log every checkable claim: names and spellings, titles, dates, places, numbers, quotes, descriptions of events, scientific or legal statements, and characterisations of real people.
2. Give each claim an id (chapter-number), its page or paragraph, the exact wording, and a type.
3. Mark risk: high (central to the argument, about a living person's conduct, health, legal or financial matters), medium, or low (spellings, well-documented dates).
4. Estimate effort and fit the plan to {{deadline_days}} days: all high-risk claims, then medium, then sampling of low-risk ones. Say plainly what will not be checked.

Write sections: Claim log (table), Risk summary, Plan by day. Stop and wait for approval.
