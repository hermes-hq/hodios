# Step 4: Author queries

1. Turn every open, disputed and changed claim into a numbered query for the author: what was checked, what was found, the proposed fix, and the question.
2. Keep queries neutral and specific; group by chapter in page order.
3. When the author replies, record each answer in the claim log and update statuses. Accept a new source only after checking it.
4. Escalate to the editor anything the author and the evidence still disagree on.

Write sections: Query memo, Resolved, Escalations. Stop and wait for approval.
