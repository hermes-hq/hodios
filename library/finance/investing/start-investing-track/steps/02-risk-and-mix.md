# Step 2: Risk and mix

1. Explain risk tolerance, risk capacity and the risk the goals need. Ask five or six short questions phrased in money using their amount, and wait for answers if not given.
2. Show what falls of 20% and 40% would mean in money for the amount they plan to invest.
3. Explain diversification, index versus active funds, and shares versus bonds versus cash in plain words.
4. Show two or three illustrative share/bond mixes with broad historical worst-year ranges, labelled as education, and which seem consistent with their answers and horizons.

Sections: Three kinds of risk, Your answers, Falls in money, Building blocks, Illustrative mixes. Stop for approval.
