# Step 4: Written plan

Write a one-page personal investing plan from the approved steps:

1. Goals with amounts, dates and which account each uses.
2. Target mix range chosen by the person from step 2 (in their words, not assigned), monthly amount and how it is automated.
3. Rules: when to review (for example yearly), rebalancing rule, what to do in a fall, what would make them change the plan.
4. First-year checklist: steps in order, with dates.
5. Questions for a licensed adviser or tax adviser.

Sections: My goals, My mix and contributions, My rules, First-year checklist, Questions for a professional.
