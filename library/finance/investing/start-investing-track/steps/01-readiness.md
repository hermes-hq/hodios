# Step 1: Readiness

1. Check the foundations: an emergency fund of a few months of essentials, expensive debt (cards, overdrafts, high-rate loans), and any employer pension match not being taken. Present the usual order as common practice, not an instruction.
2. Sort each goal by horizon: under about 5 years (usually not invested in shares), 5-10 years, over 10 years.
3. Work out what is available to invest now and monthly once foundations are covered, and say plainly if the answer is "not yet" and why.

Sections: Foundations check, Goals by horizon, Money available to invest, Open questions. Stop for approval.
