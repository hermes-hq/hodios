# Step 2: Gather records

1. A checklist of what to download or collect per account and month: statements, payment-app and marketplace reports, sales invoices, supplier bills, payroll reports, loan statements, tax letters.
2. Where to find missing items: email searches for receipts, supplier portals, duplicate invoices on request, bank statement copies.
3. A simple folder and file-naming scheme by month.
4. A gap log to fill as they go: what is missing, why, and the substitute evidence (statement line plus a written note).

Sections: Collection checklist, Where to find missing items, Filing scheme, Gap log template. Stop for approval.
