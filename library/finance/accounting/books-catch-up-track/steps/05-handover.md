# Step 5: Handover and keep current

1. Build the accountant pack: period covered, accounts reconciled, profit summary per month, the gap log, the questions list, and items needing judgement (mixed-use costs, large purchases that may be assets, owner transactions).
2. Draft a short covering note to the accountant.
3. Set a routine so it does not happen again: a weekly 20-minute coding session, a monthly reconciliation date, and receipts captured on the day.

Sections: Accountant pack, Covering note, Routine from now on.
