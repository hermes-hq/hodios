# Step 4: Reconcile

1. For each account and month, reconcile book balance to statement balance; opening balance must equal the prior month's closing figure.
2. Explain each difference (timing, duplicate, missing entry, mis-keyed amount) and propose the correction; never plug.
3. Check unpaid invoices and bills lists against later payments.
4. Summarise status per month: reconciled, or open with the amount and the next check.

Sections: Reconciliation status, Differences and corrections, Open receivables and payables, Still open. Stop for approval.
