# Step 1: Scope and deadlines

1. Confirm the period to catch up, the legal form, every bank, card, loan and payment-app account, and the tool to use.
2. List filing and payment deadlines that may be affected (income tax, sales tax, payroll, company filings), each marked "confirm locally", with any already missed at the top.
3. Estimate the size of the job: months times accounts times rough transactions a month, and a realistic number of sessions.
4. Set the order of work: periods tied to the nearest deadline first, then oldest to newest.

Sections: Deadlines, Accounts in scope, Size of the job, Order of work, Open questions. Stop for approval.
