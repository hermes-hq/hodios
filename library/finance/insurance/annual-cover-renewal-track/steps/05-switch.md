# Step 5: Renew or switch

1. For each policy, a checklist in order: start the new policy on the day the old one ends (no gap), stop auto-renewal or cancel the old one in writing, cancel any direct debit only after cancellation is confirmed, and keep proof of no-claims or claims history the new insurer asks for.
2. Note cooling-off periods and cancellation fees as things to check, and what to do if the old insurer renews automatically.
3. Update any party that needs proof of cover (mortgage lender, landlord, vehicle registration or road authorities where required).
4. Add next year's renewal dates and a reminder six weeks before each to the calendar, and file the documents.
