# Step 4: Compare

1. Put the renewal offer and new quotes side by side per policy: price, sums insured, excesses (including special excesses), key limits, add-ons, and anything the quote does not mention.
2. Work one or two realistic claims through each to show what each would pay.
3. Separate quotes that are cheaper because they cover less from those that are cheaper for the same cover; for the former, state exactly what would be given up.
4. Note the total yearly saving or extra cost across the household, and any cancellation or mid-term fees if switching.

Sections: Comparison by policy, Worked claims, Cheaper for less versus cheaper for the same, Household total, Decisions for you. Stop for the person's decision.
