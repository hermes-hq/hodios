# Step 1: Policy calendar

1. List every policy with renewal date, payment method (annual, monthly, auto-renew), this year's and last year's premium and the change, cover level and excess.
2. Include cover held elsewhere: through bank accounts, cards, employers or memberships, and flag likely overlaps.
3. Order by renewal date and mark which renewals fall in the next 6 weeks; note that quotes are often best sought a few weeks before renewal, not on the day.

Sections: Policy calendar, Cover held elsewhere, Overlaps to check, Unknowns. Stop for approval.
