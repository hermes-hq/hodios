# Step 3: Re-quote brief

1. For each policy renewing soon, write a like-for-like quote brief: the confirmed facts, sums insured, excess, add-ons and the features that must not be dropped (for example accidental damage, away-from-home cover, protected no-claims discount, courtesy car, pre-existing medical conditions on travel cover).
2. List where to get quotes (direct, comparison sites, a broker) without naming providers, and what to watch for on comparison sites (default excesses, stripped add-ons).
3. Prepare a short script for the current insurer: ask for their best renewal price for the same cover, quoting the renewal notice and any lower like-for-like quote.

Sections: Quote briefs, Must-keep features, Where to quote, Script for the current insurer. Stop for approval while the person gathers quotes.
