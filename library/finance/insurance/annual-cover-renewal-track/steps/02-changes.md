# Step 2: What changed

1. Go through changes in the last year for each policy: address, occupants, home working, lodgers or short lets, building work, valuables bought, vehicles, drivers, mileage, use, health or occupation for personal cover, claims made.
2. For each change, say which policy it affects and whether the insurer must be told now rather than at renewal.
3. Check sums insured and limits still fit (rebuild cost, contents, single items, travel destinations and activities).
4. Note anything no longer needed (a sold car, a gadget policy on a phone that has been replaced).

Sections: Changes by policy, Tell the insurer now, Sums insured and limits to update, Cover possibly no longer needed. Stop for approval.
