# Step 2: Budget

1. Build a monthly income and spending budget from the money details, converting weekly and yearly items to monthly; check that every income the household may be entitled to has been considered, with benefits or credits marked to verify locally.
2. Protect essentials and current priority bills first, then show the amount left for priority arrears and then for non-priority debts.
3. Compare that amount with the contractual payments from step 1 and say plainly whether there is a surplus, a squeeze or a shortfall.
4. Suggest realistic savings only where they do not harm health, work or children.

Sections: Income, Essentials, Priority payments, Available for other debts, Gap, Open questions. Stop for approval.
