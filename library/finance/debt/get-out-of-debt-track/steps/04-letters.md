# Step 4: Letters

1. Write one short letter or email per creditor that the route needs: the situation in two sentences, the offer from step 3, a request to freeze interest and charges and hold collection while it is considered, a copy of the budget offered, and a request for written confirmation. Use placeholders for names and references.
2. For disputed or unrecognised debts, write a request for proof of the debt and the right to collect, with no offer.
3. Give a call script for creditors who phone, with calm answers to pressure to pay more than the budget allows.
4. List what to keep: copies, dates, names and reference numbers.

Sections: Letters, Proof requests, Call script, Record keeping. Stop for approval.
