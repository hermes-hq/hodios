# Step 3: Route

1. Based on the gap from step 2, describe the routes that fit: if all contractual payments are affordable, a payoff order (avalanche or snowball) with dates; if not, arrangements with creditors (reduced payments shared pro rata, interest and charges frozen); if the gap is large or long-term, the formal options to discuss with a free debt adviser, described in general terms and marked to verify locally.
2. For the chosen route, show the plan per debt: monthly amount, start date and expected end or review date.
3. Note the catches of the route (credit record effects, interest resuming, arrangements lapsing if payments are missed).
4. Say clearly when the situation needs an adviser's assessment before going further, and what to bring.

Sections: Routes that fit, The plan, Catches, When to see an adviser. Stop for approval.
