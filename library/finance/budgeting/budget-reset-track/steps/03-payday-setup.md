# Step 3: Payday set-up

1. Propose three to five accounts or pots (bills, spending, buffer, goals) re-using what they have.
2. List the payday transfers with amounts, showing they add up to take-home pay, and a bills buffer.
3. Flag bills due just before payday and suggest asking to move them.
4. Give an ordered set-up checklist, including the gap month while direct debits move.
5. Define the weekly ten-minute check.

Sections: Accounts, Payday transfers, Due dates to move, Set-up checklist, Weekly check. Stop for approval.
