# Step 1: Spending picture

1. Categorise the transactions into no more than about twelve categories. Exclude transfers between their own accounts and card repayments already counted as purchases.
2. Average each category per month and convert annual or irregular costs (insurance, gifts, car) to a monthly figure.
3. Compare average spending with take-home income and give the monthly surplus or gap.
4. Flag leaks: subscriptions, fees and interest, duplicate charges, spikes. Ask about anything you cannot classify.

Sections: Spending by category, Irregular costs, Income versus spending, Leaks to check, Open questions. Stop for approval and answers.
