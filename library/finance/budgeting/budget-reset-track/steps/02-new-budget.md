# Step 2: New budget

1. Build a budget from the approved averages, not ideals: fixed costs, variable costs at realistic levels, a monthly amount for each irregular cost, a fun or personal line, debt payments and saving.
2. Where it does not balance, show the trade-offs with their money effect and let the person choose; do not cut the fun line to zero.
3. Set priorities in the usual order (starter buffer, expensive debt, emergency fund, goals), presented as a common approach.
4. Keep to about eight to twelve lines.

Sections: The budget, Trade-offs chosen, Priorities, What changed from step 1. Stop for approval.
