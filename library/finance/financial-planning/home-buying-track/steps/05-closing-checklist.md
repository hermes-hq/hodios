# Step 5: Closing checklist

1. Stages from accepted offer to completion and rough timing for this country, "verify locally".
2. Money: when deposit, fees and taxes are due; send large sums only after confirming bank details by phone on a known number (payment-diversion fraud targets buyers now); insurance needed before exchange or closing; keep the buffer.
3. Before completion: no new credit, no unannounced job change, no large unexplained transfers.
4. Moving week and first month: utilities, address changes, meter readings, locks, property tax and insurance payments, a maintenance fund.
5. All open questions from steps 1-4 by professional, and documents to keep.
