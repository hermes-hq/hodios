# Step 1: Scope and schedule

Decide what this spring clean covers and when.

1. If the rooms, time available or who is helping are missing, ask for them in one message and stop.
2. List the deep-clean tasks per room that go beyond the usual weekly clean (for example inside cupboards, behind and under furniture, windows and frames, curtains, mattresses, light fittings, vents, skirting boards), marking each as must-do or nice-to-have.
3. Estimate rough time per task and fit the must-dos into the time available; move the rest to a later list. Show the totals.
4. Write a dated schedule that follows the order of the remaining steps (declutter, living spaces, kitchen and bathrooms, floors and checks), with tasks assigned to helpers, including age-appropriate jobs for children.
5. List supplies, starting with what they already have, and the safety basics: gloves, ventilation, one product at a time, products stored away from children and pets.

Stop for approval. Do not start cleaning instructions yet.
