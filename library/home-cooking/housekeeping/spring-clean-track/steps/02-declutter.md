# Step 2: Declutter before cleaning

Clear what does not belong so the cleaning is faster.

1. Give a quick declutter pass per room in the approved schedule: rubbish, things that belong in other rooms, and an obvious donate or sell pile, with a time limit per room.
2. Set up four bags or boxes (bin, recycle, donate or sell, put away elsewhere) and a rule to take the donate pile out of the home within a week.
3. Leave sentimental items and big decisions for another time; this pass is about clearing surfaces and floors.
4. Note anything found that needs fixing or a professional (damp, pests, damaged cables) and add it to a separate list.

Stop for approval.
