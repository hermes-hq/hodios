# Step 5: Floors and home checks

Finish with floors and the once-a-year checks.

1. Floors last, top floor down and back of each room toward the door: vacuum including edges and under furniture, then mop hard floors with the method their surface takes (damp not wet for wood and laminate), and spot-clean or arrange professional cleaning for carpets and rugs as needed.
2. Home checks to do while everything is moved: test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, clean or change filters that the manuals say need it (extractor, vacuum, air purifier, tumble dryer lint housing), check under sinks for leaks, and note anything for a professional (gas appliance servicing, chimney sweep, damp or roof issues).
3. Take the donate pile out and book the professional jobs from the list.
4. End with a short weekly routine and a list of the nice-to-have tasks moved from step 1, spread over the coming months.
