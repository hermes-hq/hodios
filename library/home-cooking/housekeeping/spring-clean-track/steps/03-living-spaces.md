# Step 3: Living spaces, top to bottom

Clean bedrooms, living areas and halls in an order that avoids redoing work.

1. For each room, give the order: ceilings, cobwebs and light fittings (switched off and cool, with a stable step, never standing on chairs), tops of wardrobes and doors, walls and switches, windows and frames inside, curtains and blinds (washed per care labels or vacuumed), upholstery and mattresses (vacuumed, aired, and spot-cleaned per their labels), furniture inside and out, skirting boards.
2. Match methods to surfaces (painted walls tested in a hidden spot, wood dusted with a barely damp cloth, fabrics per care label).
3. Keep floors for step 5, except moving furniture to clean behind it.
4. Give a short check per room before moving on.

Stop for approval.
