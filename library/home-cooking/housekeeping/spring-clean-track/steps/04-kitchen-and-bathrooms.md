# Step 4: Kitchen and bathrooms

Deep clean the wet rooms.

1. Kitchen: start slow jobs first (soaking oven racks and extractor filters, defrosting the freezer), then cupboards inside one at a time with an expired-food check, appliances switched off and unplugged, oven per its manual, fridge with food kept cold, splashback and worktops, sink and drain, bins.
2. Bathrooms: limescale and soap scum with a product suitable for each surface, grout, toilet, shower head, drains cleared mechanically first, extractor grille; mould inside silicone means replacing the sealant.
3. Never mix products. Keep acid tasks (limescale) and any bleach task separate, rinse well and ventilate between them, and never add a second product to a toilet bowl that already contains one.
4. Respect surface limits: no acids on natural stone, no abrasives on acrylic, stainless steel, glass hobs or gloss units.

Stop for approval.
