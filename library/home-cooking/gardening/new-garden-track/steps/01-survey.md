# Step 1: Survey what is there

1. Inventory the garden by area: trees, shrubs, borders, lawn, hard surfaces, structures (sheds, fences, walls, steps), water (taps, butts, drains), and anything hazardous (broken glass, rubble, unstable walls, old wiring).
2. Mark each plant as known, probably (with the features that would confirm it) or unknown.
3. If the garden is newly taken on, recommend watching it through a season before removing anything not clearly dead or hazardous, because bulbs and perennials appear later.
4. Flag anything that needs care straight away: hazards, possible invasive species, and trees that may need an arborist.

Sections: Inventory, Plants to identify, Hazards and urgent items, Open questions.
