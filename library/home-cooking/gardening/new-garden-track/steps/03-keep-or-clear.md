# Step 3: Keep, improve or clear

1. For each item in the inventory, decide: keep, improve (prune, divide, move, repair) or clear, with the reason linked to the conditions and wishes.
2. Order the clearing: hazards first, then perennial weeds by method and season, then overgrown shrubs with renovation pruning spread over seasons where it suits the plant.
3. Timing rules: bird nesting season for hedges and shrubs, the right pruning season, and waiting for unknown plants to show themselves.
4. Waste: what can be composted, what must not be (perennial weed roots, diseased material, invasive species), and disposal options to check locally.

Sections: Keep, improve or clear table, Clearing order, Timing, Waste, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
