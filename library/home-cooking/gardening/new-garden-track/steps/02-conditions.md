# Step 2: Site conditions

1. Sun: map sun and shade across the day and how it changes by season; ask the gardener to note it if unknown.
2. Soil: a simple texture test (squeeze test), drainage (a dig-and-fill hole test), pH with a home kit, and signs from existing plants and weeds.
3. Climate and microclimate: region, frost dates, wind exposure, frost pockets, dry spots by walls, wet hollows.
4. Water: where water collects and where it is hard to reach with a hose.

Sections: Sun and shade map, Soil, Climate and microclimates, Water, Open questions.
