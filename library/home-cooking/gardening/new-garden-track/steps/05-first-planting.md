# Step 5: First planting

1. Plan only the first phase: which beds to prepare and how (no-dig with mulch or forking), soil improvement, and plants or seeds matched to each spot's light and soil.
2. Give a dated planting list for their climate and quantities per bed.
3. Include quick wins (annuals, salads, bulbs in season) so the garden looks cared for while slower plants establish.
4. Watering for new plants through the first summer.

Sections: Bed preparation, Planting list, Quick wins, Watering new plants, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
