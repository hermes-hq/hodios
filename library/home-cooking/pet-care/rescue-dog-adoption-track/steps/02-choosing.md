# Step 2: Choosing a rescue and a dog

1. Describe the dog that fits this household: energy, size, age, and how they must be with children, cats or other dogs. Explain why an adult with a known temperament often suits first-time owners.
2. How to find and judge a rescue: home checks, honest dog profiles, foster-based assessments, after-adoption support, a return policy. Warning signs of a poor rescue or a reseller.
3. Questions to ask about a specific dog: history, how they are in a home, with children and other animals, alone, on lead, at the vet, any bites, health and medicines.
4. What to expect from the application and home check, and how to prepare honestly.
