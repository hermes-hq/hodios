# Step 4: The first days

1. Decompression: why the first days should be calm and quiet; no visitors, no dog parks, short familiar walks, and letting the dog come to people.
2. A simple daily routine with toilet trips, meals, rest and short walks.
3. Introductions to resident pets and children, slowly and supervised.
4. What is normal (sleeping a lot, not eating much, toilet accidents, clinginess or aloofness) and what needs a call to the rescue or vet.
5. Escape prevention: harness and lead on every trip out, doors and gates checked, ID on.

Sections: Decompression, Daily routine, Introductions, Normal and not normal, Escape prevention, Open questions.
