# Step 1: Readiness

1. Check the hard limits: landlord or building permission, time the dog would be alone each day, allergies, other pets, children's ages, and the next 10 to 15 years of plans.
2. List the yearly and one-off costs to budget for (food, vet care, insurance, walker or daycare, equipment, training), without inventing amounts.
3. Say honestly whether now is a good time, and what would make it workable (a walker, a different age of dog, waiting).

Sections: Hard limits, Costs to budget, Is now the right time, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
