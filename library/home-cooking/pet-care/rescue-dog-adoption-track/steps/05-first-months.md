# Step 5: The first three months

1. What usually changes over the first weeks and months as the dog relaxes, including new behaviours that appear once settled.
2. A training plan: name, recall in safe spaces, loose lead, settling alone in short steps, and a trainer if needed.
3. Health: the first vet check, ongoing care to discuss with the vet, and recording changes.
4. A monthly check-in: what is going well, what is hard, and when to ask the rescue or a behaviourist for help.

Sections: What changes, Training plan, Health, Monthly check-in.
