# Step 3: Preparing the home

1. Supplies list: bed, crate or pen if suitable, harness and lead, ID tag, bowls, the food the dog is used to, chews and toys, baby gates, enzyme cleaner.
2. Dog-proofing: secure garden and gates, hazards indoors, a quiet base room.
3. A family agreement: who feeds, walks and trains, house rules, cue words everyone uses, and how children behave around the new dog.
4. Book a vet registration and first check, and a reward-based trainer if wanted.
5. Plan the pickup day: travel, a direct route home, a quiet arrival.

Sections: Supplies, Dog-proofing, Family agreement, Bookings, Pickup day, Open questions.
