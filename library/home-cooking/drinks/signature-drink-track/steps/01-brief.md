# Step 1: Brief

Agree what the drink must be before writing a recipe.

Occasion: {{occasion}}
Tastes and limits: {{tastes_and_limits}}

1. If the guest count, season, flavour likes or equipment are missing, ask for them in one message and stop.
2. Write a brief: the feeling and story of the drink, flavour direction (for example bright and citrusy, bitter and aromatic, rich and spiced), serve (long or short, glass, ice), target strength (moderate), and practical limits (prep time, equipment, budget, allergens).
3. Offer three short concepts that fit the brief, each with its template family (sour, highball, spritz, stirred, punch), main flavours and why it suits the occasion.
4. Note who needs the alcohol-free version (drivers, pregnant guests, under-age guests, non-drinkers) so it is planned from the start.

Stop for approval of one concept.
