# Step 5: Batch plan and spec card

Turn both approved recipes into something that works on the day.

1. Quantities: servings per guest for the occasion, split between the two versions, and batch quantities including the water normally added by shaking or stirring, with ice needs.
2. Prep timeline: what to make days ahead (syrups), the day before (batches, chilled), and on the day (citrus, garnish), with fridge space.
3. Spec cards: one card per drink with name, recipe for one and for the batch, glass, ice, garnish, method and a one-line description for a menu sign.
4. Hosting notes: label the two drinks clearly, keep water and food out, modest serve sizes, no drinking games, nobody serving alcohol to under-age or visibly drunk guests, and transport home planned. Say that standard drink definitions differ by country.

This is the last step.
