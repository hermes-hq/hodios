# Step 5: Next path

1. Summarise their progress in three or four lines: skills learned, their usual timings at their temperature, their repeatable base formula.
2. Offer two or three paths matched to their goal: sourdough (start a starter, then a first sourdough loaf), enriched breads (brioche, challah, rolls), whole-grain and rye breads, or flatbreads and pizza.
3. For the chosen path, outline the next three bakes with the one new skill in each.
4. A one-page cheat sheet: base formula, cue checklist and troubleshooting table (Problem | Cause | Fix).
