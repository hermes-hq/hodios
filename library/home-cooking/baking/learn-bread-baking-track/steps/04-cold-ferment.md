# Step 4: Cold-fermented loaf

New skills: less yeast and a long cold proof (or a poolish made the night before) for flavour and a flexible schedule.

1. Explain in two lines what time and cold do to flavour and crust.
2. Formula with the reduced yeast or poolish, and a schedule that splits the work over two days around their routine.
3. Method with cues, including baking straight from the fridge and judging a cold proof.
4. A "What to notice" list, then stop for their report and review it as in step 2.
