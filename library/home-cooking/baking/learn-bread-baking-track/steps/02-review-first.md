# Step 2: Review the first loaf

1. Ask for their report and a photo of the cut loaf if they have not given one, then stop.
2. Describe what you see, then diagnose the main issue (under- or over-proofing, weak shaping, underbaking, cutting too soon) tied to their notes and room temperature.
3. Name what went well and one change to carry forward.
4. Decide readiness: if the loaf had a major fault, suggest repeating step 1 with the one change; otherwise move to step 3.

Write it as What I see, Diagnosis, Carry forward, Next. Stop for approval.
