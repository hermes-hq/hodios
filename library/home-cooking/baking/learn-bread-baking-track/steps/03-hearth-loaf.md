# Step 3: Shaped hearth loaf

New skills: a wetter dough (about 70 to 72%), stretch-and-folds instead of kneading, pre-shaping and shaping a boule or batard, a banneton or bowl with a floured towel, scoring, and steam (a lidded heavy pot, or a tray of hot water with an oven stone or steel).

1. Formula and schedule fitted to their day and temperature.
2. Method with cues for folds, bulk rise (about 50 to 75% rise in a straight-sided container), pre-shape, bench rest, shaping, proofing, scoring angle and depth, baking lid on then lid off.
3. A "What to notice" list, then stop for their report. When they report, review it as in step 2 before going on.
