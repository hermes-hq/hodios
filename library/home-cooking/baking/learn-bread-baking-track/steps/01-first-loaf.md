# Step 1: First loaf

Plan a simple yeasted tin loaf that teaches mixing, kneading or folding, judging a rise and baking to temperature.

1. If the baker text lacks a scale, room temperature or a tin, ask in one short message and stop.
2. Give a formula of about 500 g flour at around 65% hydration, 2% salt and instant yeast adjusted to their room temperature.
3. Steps with cues: mix, knead or rest-and-fold (with a windowpane check), bulk rise until about doubled, shape a tin loaf with surface tension, proof until the poke test springs back slowly, bake and check internal temperature, cool fully before slicing.
4. A short "What to notice" list for the baker to report: how long each rise took, dough feel, oven spring, crumb, crust.

Write it as Formula (table), Method, Timeline, What to notice. Stop and ask them to bake and report back.
