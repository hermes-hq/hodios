# Step 1: Value the full package

1. Restate the offer as a table: every component, the stated value, how certain it is (guaranteed, target, discretionary, illiquid), and whether it is in writing.
2. Estimate the first-year and typical-year total value with the assumptions visible (bonus at target or historical payout if known, equity at a cautious value with the vesting schedule and cliff, pension or retirement match, insurance, leave, allowances).
3. Hidden costs and terms: commute, relocation, probation length, notice period, non-compete or non-solicit, repayment clauses, on-call, travel, a lost bonus or unvested equity at the current employer.
4. Questions to send the employer to fill gaps, grouped and polite.
5. If the deadline is too short, a short message asking for more time.

Output: package table, value estimate with assumptions, terms to check, questions, optional time-request message.

Stop for approval.
