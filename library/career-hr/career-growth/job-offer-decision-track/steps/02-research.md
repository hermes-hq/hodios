# Step 2: Research the job behind the offer

1. What to find out about the role (scope, first-six-month expectations, why it is open), the manager (style, tenure, how they develop people), the team (size, turnover, workload), and the company (direction, financial health, recent layoffs or reorganisations).
2. Where to find each answer, and how to ask: a follow-up call with the hiring manager, a conversation with a future peer, people who left, public reviews (read for patterns, not single posts), the company's own announcements or filings.
3. Questions to ask in a follow-up call, phrased so they are easy to answer honestly.
4. Red flags that would change the decision, and green flags.
5. After the person reports back, summarise findings against their priorities.

Output: research plan, questions, red and green flags; after findings, a short summary.

Stop for approval.
