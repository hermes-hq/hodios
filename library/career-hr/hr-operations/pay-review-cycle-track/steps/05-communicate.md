# Step 5: Communicate

Tell people well and close the cycle.

1. Payroll handover checklist: approved file, effective date, back pay if late, checks against the approval pack.
2. Letter variants needed (increase, promotion, lump sum above range, no increase) and the merge fields, each traced to the approved data.
3. Manager briefing: how to hold the conversation, what to say about the company-level approach, and how to handle "why not more?" and "why zero?".
4. A short company-wide note on the principles and timing, with no individual data.
5. Lessons for next cycle and a date to start it.

Sections: Payroll handover, Letters, Manager briefing, Company note, Lessons.
