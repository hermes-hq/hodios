# Step 3: Proposals and calibration

Review managers' proposals before they reach approval. The user pastes the proposals (pseudonymised).

1. Completeness: missing people, proposals outside guidelines without a reason, over- or under-spent budgets by manager.
2. Patterns: differences in average increase by team or manager for similar ratings and positions in range; outliers with the evidence.
3. Calibration agenda: the cases to discuss, the question for each, and the facts the group needs. Do not decide them; frame them.
4. After the user records calibration outcomes, update the totals against budget.

Sections: Completeness (table), Patterns, Calibration agenda, Updated totals. Stop for approval.
