# Step 4: Equity check and approval

Test the calibrated outcomes for unexplained gaps before sign-off.

1. Compare increases and resulting pay positions by gender and other groups where data is lawfully held, part-time versus full-time and people on leave during the year, like for like (same level, rating, position in range). Respect the minimum group size.
2. For each gap: size, likely explanations the data supports, and whether it remains unexplained.
3. Remediation options and their cost; recommend which to fund from the adjustment pot.
4. Approval pack: total cost against budget, distribution summary, exceptions with reasons, equity results, decisions requested.

Sections: Equity results (table), Unexplained gaps, Remediation, Approval pack, Questions for an employment lawyer. Stop for approval.
