# Step 1: Budget and principles

Agree what this year's pay money is for before anyone proposes a number.

1. Last cycle: what was spent, by rating and position in range, and problems reported (from the inputs; mark gaps).
2. Pots: merit, promotions, market or equity adjustments, statutory or collectively agreed increases. Recommend whether they are separate and why.
3. Budget scenarios: two or three totals with the cost of each pot, shown as a percentage of eligible payroll with the working.
4. Principles: three to five plain statements leadership signs off (for example "people low in range for their level move faster").
5. Eligibility: cut-off dates, new joiners, people on leave (who stay eligible), part-timers.

Sections: Last cycle, Pots, Budget scenarios (table), Principles, Eligibility, Questions to confirm locally. Stop for approval.
