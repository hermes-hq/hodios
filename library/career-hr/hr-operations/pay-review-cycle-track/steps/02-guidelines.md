# Step 2: Guidelines

Turn the approved budget into guidelines managers can follow.

1. Increase grid: rating by position-in-range band, each cell a target and a range. Cost it against the population distribution and the merit pot; adjust until it fits, showing the working.
2. Rules: above band maximum, promotions in the cycle, minimum and maximum increases, rounding, lump sums.
3. Manager pack outline: their budget, the grid, a worksheet with columns to fill, the deadline, and what needs a written reason (any increase outside the cell range or a zero for an eligible person).
4. Timeline back from the effective date: manager proposals, calibration, approval, payroll cut-off, letters.

Sections: Grid (table), Cost check, Rules, Manager pack outline, Timeline. Stop for approval.
