# Step 2: Records and contracts

Put the foundations in order from the approved baseline.

1. Employee record: the fields to hold for each person (identity and contact, contract terms, pay, leave, emergency contact, right-to-work evidence where required), where they live, who can access them, and what not to collect. Mark retention periods "confirm locally".
2. Contracts: which people need a written contract or statement of terms now, the clauses to check exist (role, pay, hours, place of work, leave, notice, probation, confidentiality, IP), and a plan to issue or update them. This is a checklist for a lawyer or template provider, not a drafted contract.
3. Worker status: for any flagged misclassification, the facts to gather and the question to put to an adviser.
4. Data protection: a staff privacy notice, access control on HR files and a secure place for sensitive data, all to confirm against local rules.

Sections: Employee record (table: Field | Why | Access | Retention to confirm), Contracts plan, Worker status questions, Data protection actions, Questions for an employment lawyer, What to bring next. Stop for approval.
