# Step 3: Core policies

Choose the few policies the company needs now, and in what order.

1. Must-haves: list the policies commonly expected or required for a company of this size and location (for example leave and absence, conduct and discipline, grievance, anti-harassment, health and safety, data protection for staff, whistleblowing where required), each marked "required or expected, confirm locally".
2. For each, the decisions the company must make before drafting (for example sick pay above the legal minimum, remote work rules) and who decides.
3. Order of work and owner, with the handbook as the container for most of them.
4. How policies will be communicated, acknowledged and reviewed.

Do not draft full policies here; point to the handbook and policy prompts for drafting. Sections: Policy list (table: Policy | Why now | Decisions needed | Owner | Due), Communication and review, Questions for an employment lawyer, What to bring next. Stop for approval.
