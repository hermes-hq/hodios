# Step 4: Lifecycle processes

Design the processes that run from hire to exit, lightly enough for the company's size.

1. Hire to start: offer, contract issue, right-to-work check where required, payroll and system set-up, first-day plan.
2. Probation and early reviews: checkpoints and who owns them.
3. Everyday: leave requests and approval, absence reporting, changes to terms, expenses.
4. Performance and pay: when reviews happen and how pay decisions are made and recorded.
5. Concerns: how an employee raises a concern and who handles it independently.
6. Exit: resignation handling, final pay items to confirm, access removal and records.

For each, a short flow with owner, the record kept and the tool used (spreadsheet or system). Sections: one subsection per process, then Tools needed, Questions for an employment lawyer, What to bring next. Stop for approval.
