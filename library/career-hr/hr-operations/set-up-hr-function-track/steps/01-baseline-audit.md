# Step 1: Baseline audit

Find out what exists and where the company is exposed before building anything.

1. People inventory: how many people by country, worker type and contract type, with gaps marked. Flag anyone whose status may be misclassified (for example a "contractor" working fixed hours under direction), as a point to confirm locally.
2. Document check: for each area (contracts, offer letters, handbook or policies, payroll records, leave records, right-to-work or work permit checks, personnel files, data protection notices to staff), say what exists, what is missing and how urgent it is.
3. Exposure list: the five to eight gaps that carry the most legal, financial or people risk, ranked, with why.
4. Quick wins: two or three fixes that take under a week.

Sections: People inventory (table), Document check (table: Area | Exists? | Gap | Urgency), Exposure list, Quick wins, Questions for an employment lawyer, What to bring next. Stop for approval.
