# Step 1: Paperwork and deposit

1. List what must be in writing before work starts: scope and deliverables, price and payment schedule, timeline, revision limit, IP terms, and the contract or terms of business it sits under. Mark each as done, drafted or missing.
2. Flag gaps or risks in the deal (no deposit, long payment terms, vague deliverables, no named decision-maker) with a suggested fix for each.
3. Write the message that sends the agreement and the deposit invoice, with a target start date that depends on both being returned.
4. Note supplier onboarding or purchase-order steps the client may need, if the client is a larger organisation.

Sections: Paperwork status, Gaps and fixes, Message, Client admin. Stop and wait for approval.
