# Step 4: Two-week check-in

Ask the freelancer what has happened since kickoff: inputs received or late, feedback pattern, extra requests, payment status, how the working relationship feels. Do not assume.

1. Compare reality with the plan: inputs, timeline, scope, communication, payment. Mark each on track, drifting or off track.
2. For each drifting item, propose a fix and the wording to raise it (a timeline reset, a scope-change note with a price, a reminder of feedback rules, a payment reminder).
3. Write a short check-in message to the client that confirms progress, raises at most two issues, and sets the next milestone.
4. Note what to change in the freelancer's onboarding for the next client.

Sections: Status, Fixes, Check-in message, Lessons for next time.
