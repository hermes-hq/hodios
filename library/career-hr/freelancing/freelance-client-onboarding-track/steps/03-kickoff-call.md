# Step 3: Kickoff call

1. Write a 30 to 45 minute agenda: goals and success measures in the client's words, audience, deliverables walk-through, timeline and milestones, who gives feedback and how (one consolidated round per stage), communication hours and channels, and what happens when something changes.
2. List the questions to ask that the proposal left open.
3. Draft the follow-up recap email to send the same day: decisions, actions with owners and dates, next milestone, and anything still open.
4. If the freelancer has a welcome pack, say where it fits; if not, list the points the recap must cover instead.

Sections: Agenda, Open questions, Recap email, Welcome pack note. Stop and wait for approval.
