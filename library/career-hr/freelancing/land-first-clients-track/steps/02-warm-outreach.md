# Step 2: Warm network outreach

1. Sort the network into three groups: could hire you, could refer you, could advise you. Ask for roles if the network list is empty.
2. Write a short personal message for each group (under 90 words): what you now do, for whom, and one specific ask (a 15-minute catch-up, an introduction, feedback on the offer). No attachments or long pitch.
3. A forwardable two-line blurb for referrers.
4. A tracker: name or role, group, date sent, reply, next step. A follow-up after a week, once.

Sections: Network groups, Messages, Forwardable blurb, Tracker (table). Stop and wait for approval.
