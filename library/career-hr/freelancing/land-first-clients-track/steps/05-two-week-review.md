# Step 5: Two-week review

Ask for the tracker results: messages sent, replies, calls, proposals, wins and what people said.

1. Funnel table by channel (warm, referral, cold): sent, replies, calls, proposals, won, with rates.
2. What worked and what did not, using the replies; change one thing (offer wording, target, message, price) at a time.
3. The plan for the next two weeks, within the hours available, and a referral ask for any happy client.

Sections: Funnel (table), What to change, Next two weeks.
