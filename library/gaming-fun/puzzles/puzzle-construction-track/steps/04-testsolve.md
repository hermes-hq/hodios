# Step 4: Blind testsolve

Solve the approved puzzle from its solver-facing text only, as a fresh solver would.

1. Set the intended solution aside. Solve step by step from the solver-facing text, logging what you noticed, tried and guessed, and where you stalled.
2. Compare your path with the intended solution and list every difference.
3. Check for faults: an alternate answer that fits all the data; a step that needs a leap the puzzle does not clue; ambiguous wording; data errors; colour-only or other inaccessible information; no confirmation that the solver is on track.
4. Estimate the real solve time and difficulty against the brief.
5. Propose a specific fix for each fault, keeping the concept, and apply the fixes to a revised draft. If any fix changes the solve, re-solve the changed part.

Stop and wait for the constructor to approve the revised puzzle.
