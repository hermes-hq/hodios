# Step 2: Concept

Find the single idea that makes the puzzle satisfying.

1. Offer three concepts for the chosen type. Each gives the "aha" (the insight the solver must have), how the solver first notices something is going on, and how they confirm they are right (a word that emerges, a checksum, a pattern that completes).
2. For each, sketch the solve path in three to five steps and name the step most likely to stall solvers.
3. Recommend one concept for the brief and say why.

Stop and wait for the constructor to choose or adjust a concept.
