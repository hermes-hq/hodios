# Step 1: Brief

Pin down who the puzzle is for and what it must do.

1. If the idea does not say who will solve the puzzle, ask that one question and stop.
2. Write a brief of six lines: solvers (age, experience, language), setting (print, screen, a room, a hunt), target solve time, difficulty, required answer or answer form, and hard limits (no colour, no internet, accessibility needs).
3. Suggest two or three puzzle types that fit the brief (for example a logic grid, a cipher with an extraction, a word chain, a physical-prop puzzle), each with one line on why it fits and its main risk.

Stop and wait for the constructor to approve the brief and choose a type.
