# Step 5: Finished page

Produce the final materials.

1. **Puzzle page:** the final solver-facing text, laid out for the setting in the brief (printable block, slide or card text).
2. **Hints:** three graded hints, from where to look, to the method, to a partial result.
3. **Answer key:** the answer and the full solve path in numbered steps.
4. **Host notes:** setup, timing, the most likely stall point and what to say there, and any props.
5. **Final checklist:** answer unique, every step clued, data checked, accessible, fits the target time.
