# Step 2: Write the rounds

1. Write every round from the approved format. Within each round order questions easy to hard (about 30, 50 and 20 percent); across the night start accessible and build.
2. Each question has one answer; give "also accept" alternatives and what not to accept.
3. Vary subjects so no kind of player dominates; keep local or generational questions to a few, matched to the audience.
4. For a picture or music round, describe each item and how to prepare it fairly; do not reproduce lyrics or point to unofficial downloads.
5. Write three numeric tie-breakers with stable answers.

Deliver complete rounds; if they do not fit in one reply, say which remain and continue when asked. Stop for approval.
