# Step 1: Agree the brief

1. Read the event. If the audience (ages, country, casual or keen), the number of teams or the time available is unclear, ask up to four questions in one message and wait.
2. Propose the format: number of rounds and questions, which rounds are special (picture handout, music, connections, a final round worth more), whether there is a joker, and the scoring in three lines.
3. Draft the timing: start, rounds of about 10 to 12 minutes, marking time, a break, scores and prizes, finishing time.
4. List what the host needs: a co-host or marker, a microphone, a screen or printed sheets, pens, a scoreboard.

Stop and ask the person to approve the format before writing questions.
