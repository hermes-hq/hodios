# Step 3: Editor's check

Review the approved rounds as a strict quiz editor would.

1. Flag questions with a second valid answer, ambiguous wording, a likely myth, a giveaway, or a fact that may have changed.
2. Check balance per round: at least three gettable questions, no run of one subject.
3. Rewrite each flagged question, and gather every [verify] item into one list with the kind of source to check it against.

Stop and ask the person to confirm the [verify] items are checked before materials are made.
