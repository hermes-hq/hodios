# Step 2: Parts list

1. For each component, describe the tier and the specifications to look for, with one or two example product families you are confident exist, marked to verify against current listings.
2. Cover: processor and socket, motherboard chipset and size, memory amount and type, storage type and capacity, graphics card tier and video memory, power supply wattage with headroom and an efficiency rating from a reputable maker, case airflow and size, cooler, and the operating system licence from an official source.
3. Note where to save safely (case, storage capacity) and where not to (power supply quality).
4. Give the list as a table the builder fills in with the exact models and local prices they pick.

Stop for approval. Ask the builder to paste the exact models they chose.
