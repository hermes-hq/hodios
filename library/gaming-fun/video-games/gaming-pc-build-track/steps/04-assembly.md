# Step 4: Assembly

1. Prepare: clear table off carpet, good light, a Phillips screwdriver, the motherboard and case manuals open. Discharge static by touching bare metal on the case or a grounded object, handle boards by their edges, and keep parts in their anti-static bags until used.
2. Electrical safety: keep the power supply switched off and unplugged from the wall whenever your hands are inside the case. Never open the power supply itself; it holds a dangerous charge even when unplugged. If it has a voltage selector, set it for your country's mains before first use.
3. Build order on the box before the case: processor, cooler with thermal paste if not pre-applied, memory in the slots the manual names, the main storage drive.
4. Into the case: standoffs matching the board, the I/O shield if separate, board, power supply, graphics card, front-panel connectors by the manual, fans for front intake and rear or top exhaust.
5. Cable checks: main power, processor power and graphics power fully seated with no gap or bend at the plug; use the power supply's own cables (never mix modular cables from another unit) and avoid adapters for graphics power unless they came with the card.
6. Common mistakes to avoid: missing standoffs, memory not clicked in, display cable plugged into the motherboard instead of the graphics card, forgotten processor power cable.

Stop for approval. Ask the builder to confirm each cable check before powering on.
