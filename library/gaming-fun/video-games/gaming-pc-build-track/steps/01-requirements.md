# Step 1: Requirements and budget split

1. Restate the goals as a performance target: resolution, target frame rate, the most demanding game on the list, and any non-gaming workloads.
2. Ask at most five questions that change the build: country, what the budget covers, size and noise preferences, upgrade plans, Wi-Fi need, and comfort with building.
3. Explain which part sets the target: for most gaming at higher resolutions the graphics card, for high-refresh competitive play or simulation-heavy games also the processor.
4. Propose a budget split as percentages (graphics card, processor, motherboard, memory, storage, power supply, case and cooling, operating system), adjusted for reused parts.
5. Say honestly if the target does not fit the budget and offer the trade-off (lower target, used parts, saving longer).

Stop for approval of the target and split.
