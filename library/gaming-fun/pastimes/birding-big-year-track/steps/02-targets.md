# Step 2: Target list

Build the list of species to plan around.

1. Ask the birder to paste or describe the area's species list from a regional checklist or recent annual report, or to name the groups they know are present. Work from that; do not invent species for the area.
2. Sort the species into tiers: common residents that will come with normal birding, seasonal visitors (summer, winter, passage), scarce regulars that need a dedicated trip, and rarities that cannot be planned.
3. For each tier, note the habitat and season, and flag the hard identification groups for a {{experience}} birder (for example gulls, warblers, waders, raptors) with what to study first.
4. Highlight the species most likely to be missed if not targeted at the right time, such as short passage windows or nocturnal birds.

Present a table: Species or group | Tier | Habitat | Best months (to check) | Notes. Stop and wait for approval.
