# Step 4: Trip plan

Plan the next outing or trip. Repeat this step as often as needed.

1. Ask for the date, the place or habitat, the target species still needed and the time available.
2. Plan the timing (dawn or dusk, tide state for waders, weather that brings migrants), a route, and the order of sites.
3. For each target, the key features and calls to check, the confusion species, and how to confirm the identification.
4. Ethics and access for the sites: stay on paths, keep distance from nests and roosts, check access rules, and no playback where harmful or banned.
5. Kit and logistics, and what to record: species, numbers, time, place and any photos or notes for rarities.

Stop and wait for the birder to approve or adjust before the trip.
