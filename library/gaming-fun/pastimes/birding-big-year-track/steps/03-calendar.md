# Step 3: Seasonal calendar

Turn the targets into a 12-month plan within {{constraints}}.

1. Lay out month by month: which tiers and species to prioritise, which habitats to visit, and key events (dawn chorus, spring and autumn passage, winter roosts, seabird season), with timings marked "to check" against local records.
2. Spread effort so the January surge, spring passage and autumn passage get enough time, and quiet months are used for scarce residents and skills study.
3. Fit trips to the budget and travel rules, and note what to drop if time runs short.
4. Add a weekly rhythm: a regular patch visit, checking local bird news, and logging.

Present a month table: Month | Priorities | Places or habitats | Effort. Stop and wait for approval.
