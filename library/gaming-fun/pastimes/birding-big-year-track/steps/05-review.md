# Step 5: Monthly review

Review progress and adjust the plan.

1. Ask for the current list, species added this month, and time and money spent.
2. Compare with the calendar: what is on track, what was missed and whether it can still be found later in the year.
3. Re-rank the remaining targets by chance and effort, and update the next month's plan.
4. Check the budget and the birder's energy; suggest cuts before burnout.
5. Note any records that need submitting to the local recorder or records committee.

Present: Progress summary, Missed and still possible, Next month's priorities, Records to submit. Return to step 4 for the next trip.
