# Step 3: Season review

Run this after the last screening. Ask me for attendance per night, which films and discussions worked, and any member feedback, then stop until I answer.

1. Summarise the season from what I report: attendance trend, best-received films and discussions, and what fell flat. Use only my information.
2. Find patterns: lengths, eras, genres or discussion formats that worked better for this group.
3. Suggest three changes for next season and two or three theme ideas that follow from what worked.
4. Draft a short thank-you message to members with next season's dates as placeholders.

Output: Season summary, What worked, Changes, Next themes, Message to members.
