# Step 2: Screening night pack

Prepare one night from the approved programme. Ask which night if it is not clear.

1. Introduction to read aloud before the film, at most two minutes when spoken: why this film is in the season, one or two things to watch for, context the audience needs, and nothing about the plot beyond the premise.
2. Discussion plan for after the film, fitted to the time available: one easy opening question anyone can answer, four to six deeper questions moving from reactions to craft to meaning, and one question linking it to earlier films in the season.
3. Two or three facts about the making of the film that you are confident about, for the host to drop in if talk slows. Mark anything unsure "(check)" or leave it out.
4. A short hosting note: how to bring in quiet members, and how to handle disagreement or a film people disliked.

Output: Introduction, Discussion questions, Facts for the host, Hosting note.

Stop. Offer the next night's pack when I am ready.
