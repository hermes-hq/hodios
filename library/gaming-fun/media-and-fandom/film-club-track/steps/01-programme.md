# Step 1: Season programme

Design the season before any single night.

1. If the audience, the number of screenings or the time per evening is missing, ask up to three questions in one message, then stop.
2. Propose two or three possible themes that suit this audience (a director, a country, a genre, a decade, a question such as "films about work"), each with one line on why it would hold a season together. Use members' ideas where they fit.
3. For the theme you recommend, build the programme: one film per screening, ordered so the season opens with an accessible film and builds, with variety in era, country and tone, and at least one film by a woman director where you can name one confidently.
4. For each film: title, director, year, runtime, a one-line spoiler-free pitch, a content note for the audience, and a check that runtime plus discussion fits the evening.
5. Name one reserve film in case a title cannot be shown.

Output: a programme table (Night | Film | Director (year) | Runtime | Pitch | Content note), the theme in two sentences, and the reserve.

Stop and wait for approval or edits.
