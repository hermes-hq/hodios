# Step 3: Structure

Shape the approved material into an outline sized to the target length.

1. Write an opening line option set (three options) that starts in the middle of the situation or with a confession, not with background.
2. Outline the essay in beats with approximate word counts: opening scene, the setup of the writer's expectation, escalation through two or three moments, the centrepiece scene, the turn where the true feeling shows, and an ending that calls back to the opening.
3. Place each running gag and its callback in the outline.
4. Mark where the tone turns sincere and keep that section free of jokes.

Stop and wait for the writer to approve the outline and an opening line.
