# Step 5: Punch-up and cut

Tighten the approved draft for publication.

1. Cut: remove explanation after punchlines, repeated jokes, throat-clearing openings and any paragraph the essay works without; aim for 10 to 15 percent shorter unless already at length.
2. Punch up: for the five weakest jokes, give a stronger alternative each, marked so the writer can choose.
3. Check the callbacks land and the sincere turn is not undercut by a joke straight after it.
4. Check fairness: everyone else in the story would recognise themselves and not feel mocked; private details are gone.
5. Deliver the final draft, the word count, three headline options and the list of remaining [CHECK] items.
