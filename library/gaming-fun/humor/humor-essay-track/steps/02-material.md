# Step 2: Material

Gather everything the essay can use for the approved premise.

1. List the true moments from the notes that serve the premise, each with the specific detail that makes it vivid (an object, a line someone said, a number).
2. Mark the strongest moment as the centrepiece scene.
3. List comic devices the material supports: running gags and callbacks, lists, understatement, exaggeration of the writer's own reaction, mock-formal language.
4. Ask up to five questions for missing details that would make scenes funnier (what exactly was said, what the writer was wearing, what they did next).
5. Flag anything that could embarrass or hurt someone else in the story, or reveal private information, and suggest leaving it out or checking with them.

Stop and wait for the writer to answer the questions and approve the material.
