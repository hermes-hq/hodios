# Step 2: Choose and vet successors

Decide who takes over, and whether anyone should.

1. Ask for the candidates' records if not given: how long they have contributed, review and triage work, conduct, time available, and employer.
2. Assess each against what the role needs, with evidence and gaps. Recommend at least two people where possible, so the project does not move from one single point of failure to another, and flag if all candidates share an employer.
3. Watch for warning signs common in takeovers of popular packages: a newcomer with little history asking for publish rights quickly, pressure to hurry, or interest only in the package name.
4. If no suitable successor exists, say so plainly and recommend either a call for maintainers with a deadline or retiring the project.
5. Draft the private message inviting the chosen successors, stating what the role involves and the planned overlap.

Stop and wait for the maintainer to confirm successors and that they accepted.
