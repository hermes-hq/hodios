# Step 5: Step back

Close out the departing maintainer's role cleanly.

1. List the access to remove from the departing maintainer at the end of the overlap, and anything they keep (for example emeritus status without admin rights).
2. Confirm that no asset in the inventory still depends on them alone.
3. Suggest a check-in date with the successors, one or three months later, and what to review then: releases done, security reports handled, open questions.
4. Write a short personal thank-you note from the departing maintainer to the successors and the community, if they want one.

This is the last step.
