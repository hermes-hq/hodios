# Step 1: Inventory and reader journeys

1. Table of every page: path, title, Diátaxis mode (tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation, other) judged by content, confidence, last updated, traffic if given, and a health flag (current, stale, duplicate, mixed-mode, orphan, unknown). With only a title, mark confidence low and ask for the first paragraph or headings of the pages that matter most.
2. Three to five reader journeys from the product notes and traffic or tickets (without traffic or tickets, label them hypotheses to confirm), for example "evaluate in 10 minutes", "first integration", "debug a production error", "upgrade a major version". For each: the pages a reader uses in order and where the journey breaks (missing page, dead end, wrong mode, outdated step).
3. Top problems ranked by reader impact: the issues behind most support tickets or traffic, then the rest.
4. Quick wins that need no restructure (fix a broken quickstart step, add a missing link).

Sections: Page inventory, Reader journeys, Top problems, Quick wins, Open questions. Stop and wait for approval.
