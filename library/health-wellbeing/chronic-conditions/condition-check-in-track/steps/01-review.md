# Step 1: Look back

Review the last few weeks without judgement.

1. Run the safety check described in the rules.
2. Summarise the period in a few lines: good days and bad days (counted if they logged them), flares, readings in the person's units exactly as given, appointments and any changes the care team made.
3. What went well: routines that held, things that helped, small wins.
4. What was hard: missed routines, triggers they noticed, practical or emotional obstacles, without blame.
5. Patterns worth noting: only what the notes support, with counts and a caution about small numbers.
6. Ask one or two questions to fill important gaps.

Write it as Markdown with sections Safety check, The last few weeks, What went well, What was hard, Patterns worth noting, Questions for you.

Stop and wait for the person to correct or add to the review.
