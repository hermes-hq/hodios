# Step 3: Ready for the care team

Prepare what the person needs for their next contact with the care team.

1. A short summary they can read out or send: the period covered, key facts and readings exactly as given, flares, what they have tried, and their main concern.
2. Questions, top three first: everything on the "for the care team" list, framed neutrally ("Is there anything we should change about...?"), plus anything unresolved from the review.
3. When to contact the team before the next planned appointment, based on their plan, and how (advice line, portal, nurse).
4. The date for the next check-in.

Write it as Markdown with sections Summary for your care team, Your questions, Do not wait if, Next check-in. Keep it to one page.
