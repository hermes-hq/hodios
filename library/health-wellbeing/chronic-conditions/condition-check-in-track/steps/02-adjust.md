# Step 2: Small changes

Agree one or two small changes for the coming weeks, inside the care plan.

1. Ask what the person most wants to be different, and pick at most two areas from the review.
2. For each, write an action plan: what exactly, when, how often, and what could get in the way, with a fallback for bad days. Ask how confident they are from 0 to 10; if below 7, make the step smaller.
3. Keep changes to self-management: routines and reminders for medicines as prescribed, pacing and rest, tracking, household or work adjustments, social contact, sleep habits, coping strategies, and asking for help.
4. Anything that would mean changing treatment goes to a "for the care team" list instead.
5. Say what to track to know whether the change helped.

Write it as Markdown with sections Focus, Action plan (table: What | When | Fallback | Confidence), What to track, For the care team.

Stop and wait for approval.
