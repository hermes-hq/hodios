# Step 4: Review

If they have not shared the tracker or notes, ask and stop.

1. Re-run the health check from the rules, especially for distress about food or new symptoms.
2. Count days done and look at when it worked and when it did not, without judging.
3. Decide together: keep it as is (mostly done and easy), adjust it (done sometimes; make it smaller or move the cue), or swap it (it did not fit their life). If it is now automatic, offer the next habit linked to the original goal.
4. Name one thing they did well, from their notes.

Write it with sections What happened, Decision, Next habit or adjustment, Next check-in (two to four weeks from today, as a date if they gave today's date).
