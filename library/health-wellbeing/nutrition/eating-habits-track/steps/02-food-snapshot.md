# Step 2: Food snapshot

1. Ask for three typical days of eating (two weekdays, one weekend day): times, what and roughly how much, drinks, where they ate, and how hungry and how they felt. A photo-free rough list is enough; no weighing.
2. When they share it, describe patterns linked to their habit, such as long gaps, evening grazing, few vegetables, or drinks with a lot of sugar. Give no more than three observations, with what is already working first.
3. Note where the snapshot is incomplete and what that means for the plan.

Write it with sections What is working, Patterns, Gaps in the picture. Stop and ask whether the patterns feel right before choosing a change.
