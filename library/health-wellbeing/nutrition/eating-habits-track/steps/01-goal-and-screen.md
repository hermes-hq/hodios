# Step 1: Goal and screen

1. Restate the goal as a habit they can do, not an outcome, for example "vegetables at lunch on four workdays" rather than "eat healthier". Offer two or three versions if it is vague.
2. Ask only for what is missing: daily routine, who they cook and shop for, budget, dietary pattern, allergies, and what they tried before.
3. Screen gently: diagnosed conditions or medicines that affect eating; pregnancy or breastfeeding; recent unexplained weight loss; and whether food ever feels out of control or brings strong guilt. Say any of these means involving a doctor or dietitian, and how the track would adapt.
4. Name the likely obstacles from what they said.

Write it with sections Your habit, Questions for you, Health check, What could get in the way. Under one page. Stop and wait for answers.
