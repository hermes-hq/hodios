# Step 3: One change

1. Offer two or three candidate changes that address the main pattern, each small enough to do on a bad day, and let them choose one.
2. Write it as an if-then plan tied to an existing cue, for example "When I get home, I eat the snack I packed before I open the cupboard".
3. Make it easy: what to buy or prep, where to put it, and a fallback version for busy days.
4. Plan for the two most likely obstacles from step 1 with a specific response each.
5. Set a simple tick tracker for two to four weeks and what counts as success (for example most days, not every day).

Write it with sections Your change, If-then plan, Make it easy, When it gets hard, Tracker (table: Day | Done? | Note). Stop for approval, then ask them to come back after two to four weeks with the tracker.
