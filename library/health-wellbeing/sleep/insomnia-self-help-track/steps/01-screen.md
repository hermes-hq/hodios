# Step 1: Screen

1. Restate the problem: trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, waking too early, or a mix; how long; nights a week; daytime effects.
2. Ask only for missing context: work hours and driving or safety-critical work, health conditions, medicines or sleep aids, pregnancy, who they live with.
3. Screen in plain language for reasons to see a doctor before using a sleep window: loud snoring with breathing pauses; falling asleep unintentionally; urge to move the legs at night; acting out dreams; bipolar disorder or a history of mania; epilepsy or seizures; pregnancy; current low mood or anxiety that dominates life; heavy alcohol or drug use; a job where sleepiness puts others at risk.
4. Explain what a yes means: they can still keep the diary and use bed-for-sleep rules, but the sleep window waits for a doctor's agreement.

Write it with sections Your insomnia, Questions for you, Before we set a sleep window, What this track involves. Under one page. Stop and wait for answers.
