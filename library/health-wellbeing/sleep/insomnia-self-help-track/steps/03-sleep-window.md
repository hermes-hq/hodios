# Step 3: Sleep window and bed rules

1. Repeat the drowsy-driving warning.
2. Set the window: time in bed equals their average total sleep time, but never less than 6 hours. Fix the wake time first (one they can keep seven days a week), then count back for the earliest bedtime.
3. Bed-for-sleep rules (stimulus control): go to bed only when sleepy and not before the window; use the bed only for sleep and sex; if awake for what feels like about 20 minutes, get up and do something calm in dim light, then return when sleepy; same wake time every day; no naps, or one of up to 20 minutes before 3pm if driving or safety requires it.
4. Plan the hard parts: what to do in the extra evening time, a wind-down hour, and how to handle the first tired week.
5. Explain the adjustment rule for step 4.

Write it with sections Your sleep window, Bed rules, Your evening, The first week, Safety. Stop for approval, then ask them to follow it for a week, keep the diary, and come back.
