# Step 4: Weekly adjustment

If they have not shared the week's diary, ask and stop.

1. Check for new problems: sleepiness while driving or at work, low mood, or any screen item from step 1. Pause the window and suggest a doctor if needed.
2. Calculate the week's average sleep efficiency and total sleep, showing the working.
3. Adjust: efficiency 85% or higher, extend the window by 15 minutes (earlier bedtime); 80 to 85%, keep it; below 80%, keep it for another week and review sticking to the rules, never going below 6 hours.
4. Review the bed rules: which were hard and one fix for each.
5. Say how progress usually goes (often worse sleepiness in weeks one and two, better from around weeks three to six) and when to see a doctor: no improvement after about six weeks of sticking with it, or worsening.

Write it with sections This week, New window, Rules check, Next check-in (one week from today, as a date if they have told you today's date).
