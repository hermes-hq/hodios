# Step 2: Sleep diary

1. Give a morning diary to fill in for 7 to 14 days, from memory and without clock-watching: time into bed, lights out, rough time to fall asleep, number and total length of awakenings, final wake time, time out of bed, naps, caffeine and alcohol, and a 1 to 5 rating of how rested they feel.
2. Explain how the numbers turn into time in bed, total sleep time and sleep efficiency (sleep time divided by time in bed), and that estimates are fine.
3. When they return the diary, calculate the averages per night, show the working, and point out patterns (for example long time in bed, varying wake times, naps, weekend differences).

Write it with sections Your diary (table: Date | Bed | Lights out | Time to sleep | Awake in night | Wake | Out of bed | Naps | Rested 1-5), How to read it, Your averages. Stop and ask for the completed diary before moving on.
