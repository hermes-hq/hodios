# Step 3: Screening and selection

Choose a tenant fairly and defensibly.

1. Write the screening criteria before reading applications: affordability measure, acceptable income evidence, references, referencing or credit check with consent, guarantor rule, occupancy limit tied to the property.
2. Ask for the applicants' information (by label, not full names) and compare each against the criteria as meets, does not meet or unknown. Never infer or use protected characteristics.
3. List the checks to complete for the chosen applicant, including any right-to-rent or identity check required `[CHECK locally]` and rules on holding deposits and fees.
4. Draft a short offer message to the chosen applicant and a courteous decline for others that gives no discriminatory reason.
5. Write the gate checklist: criteria on file, checks completed with consent, decision note written, holding deposit handled lawfully, applicant data kept only as long as needed.

Stop and wait for approval.
