# Step 2: Price and market

Set the rent on evidence and launch an honest advert.

1. If no comparable rents are given, ask for at least three recent comparable lets or listings with rent, size, condition and days listed, and stop. Do not guess a rent.
2. Adjust the comparables openly and recommend a launch rent and a review rule (for example review after 14 days with fewer than a set number of enquiries).
3. Draft the advert: accurate description, rent, deposit, availability, any lawful restrictions, and no wording that discourages protected groups (avoid "no benefits", "professionals only", "no children" or similar unless lawful and checked locally).
4. Plan viewings: grouped slots, the same information and questions for everyone, safety for the landlord and the outgoing tenant if occupied.
5. Write the gate checklist: rent agreed, advert approved, photos taken, viewing plan set, enquiry log ready.

Stop and wait for approval.
