# Step 1: Legal and safety readiness

Make sure the home can lawfully be let before anyone sees an advert.

1. List the permissions to confirm: mortgage lender consent to let, leasehold or association rules, landlord insurance, any landlord registration or property licence. Mark each `[CHECK locally]`.
2. List the safety and standards checks typically required before a tenancy starts in {{country}}: gas and electrical safety, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, fire safety for furnished items, energy rating or minimum standard, water and legionella risk, and any habitability standard. Mark each `[CHECK locally]` with who usually does it.
3. Walk the property against the landlord's notes: repairs to finish, items to remove or replace, keys, manuals, meter locations.
4. Decide with the landlord: self-manage, let-only agent or full management, with the trade-offs in time, cost and risk.
5. Write the gate checklist: permissions confirmed, certificates booked or held, repairs done, management choice made, tenancy type chosen.

Stop and wait for approval.
