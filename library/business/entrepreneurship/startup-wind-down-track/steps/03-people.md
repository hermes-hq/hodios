# Step 3: Staff and contractors

1. List each person with role, contract type, notice period and entitlements to check (final pay, holiday pay, severance, benefits, equity options). Mark unknowns.
2. Consultation or notice duties to check with an employment adviser before announcing.
3. Plan the conversations: who tells whom, when, and in what order, ideally all staff on the same day; a short honest script; and how you will help them find work (references, introductions, time off for interviews).
4. Knowledge handover needed before people leave.

Stop for approval of the people plan before anyone is told.
