# Step 5: Closing paperwork

1. Final accounts and tax returns, deregistration for taxes and payroll, and closing bank and payment accounts after the final payments clear.
2. The formal closure application or process for the route chosen, with the professional who handles it.
3. Records to keep and for how long, and who keeps them.
4. Personal follow-up for the founder: guarantees released, personal tax questions, and time to recover.

Output the final checklist with every item's status and anything still open.
