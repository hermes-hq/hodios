# Step 2: Cash and creditors

1. List everything owed by the company: staff, tax, suppliers, landlord, loans, leases, customer prepayments and refunds, investor instruments. Mark any personal guarantees.
2. List everything owed to the company and how to collect it.
3. Build a week-by-week cash plan to the closing date.
4. Questions for the accountant or insolvency practitioner: is the company solvent, which closure route fits, what order of payment applies, and what directors must and must not do now.
5. Stop new spending and commitments except what the close needs.

Stop for approval. Ask the founder to get the solvency and route answer before step 3 pays anyone.
