# Step 4: Customers, contracts and assets

1. Customers: notice periods in contracts, refunds for prepaid services, a data export for each customer, and a shutdown date. Draft a short, clear customer notice.
2. Contracts: leases, software subscriptions, supplier and partner agreements, with termination terms and dates.
3. Assets: equipment, stock, code, domains, trademarks, customer lists. Note that selling assets, especially personal data, needs proper value and may need consent; check with the professional.
4. Data: what to delete, what to keep for legal record-keeping periods, and who holds the records.
5. Investors: a draft update explaining the decision, what happens to remaining cash, and the timeline.

Stop for approval of the notices and the asset plan.
