# Step 1: Confirm the decision

1. Summarise why closing is on the table and the evidence.
2. List the alternatives still open and whether each is realistic: a pivot, a sale or acqui-hire of the team or assets, merging, handing the product to a customer or community, a bridge from investors, or going dormant. For each, the time and cash it needs against the runway.
3. Estimate the cash needed for an orderly close (final payroll and entitlements as placeholders, notice periods, contract exits, professional fees, data and records) and compare with cash on hand. If the cash will not cover debts, flag the solvency question for urgent professional advice.
4. Set the decision deadline and who must agree (cofounders, board, investors as the documents require).

Stop for the founder to confirm the decision to wind down, or choose an alternative.
