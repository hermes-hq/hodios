# Step 2: Founding team

1. Roles needed for the first year: lead or chair, money (treasurer), records and communication (secretary), activity lead, safeguarding lead if relevant, plus the voice of people the group serves.
2. Who the founder knows for each, and where to find the rest (neighbours, the people served, local volunteer centres, faith and school networks).
3. A first meeting agenda: purpose, what each person will do, time each can give, how decisions are made, conflicts of interest.
4. A short founding statement: purpose, who it serves, how it works, values.

Sections: Roles, People, First meeting, Founding statement.

Stop and wait for approval.
