# Step 6: Closing and after

1. Closing checklist: the items that usually need completing, as checks for the lawyer to confirm - final documents, board and shareholder approvals, updated cap table, any required filings, signatures from all parties, wiring instructions verified by phone to avoid payment fraud, and confirmation that funds arrived.
2. Follow-on investors: filling the round after the lead commits, with a deadline and allocation tracking.
3. Announcement: whether and when to announce, a short announcement draft with placeholders, and who to tell first (team, existing investors, key customers).
4. Thank and update: thank-you notes to investors and everyone who made introductions, and the first investor update template (highlights, metrics, lowlights, asks) with a regular cadence.
5. Retrospective: what worked in the round, what to prepare earlier next time, and the milestones and metrics the next round will be judged on.

Final output: the closing checklist, the announcement draft, the investor update template and the retrospective.
