# Step 2: Materials

1. One-liner and short description: one sentence a stranger could repeat, plus a 3-sentence description.
2. Deck outline: 10-14 slides in an order suited to the founder's strongest evidence (lead with traction if it is strong, with insight and team if it is early): problem, solution and insight, why now, market sized bottom-up (number of buyers times price, with sources to find), product, traction, business model and unit economics, go-to-market, competition, team, the round and use of funds. For each slide: the headline as a full sentence and the evidence that goes on it. Mark missing evidence as [NEEDED: ...].
3. Teaser email: under 150 words, forwardable, for intros and cold outreach.
4. Metrics sheet: the metrics investors at this stage will ask for, with definitions so they are calculated consistently (for example monthly recurring revenue, growth rate, gross margin, retention by cohort, acquisition cost, payback), and where each number will come from.
5. Financial model check: the questions the model must answer (revenue build from drivers, hiring plan, burn, runway after the round, milestones by month), and what an accountant or finance lead should review.
6. Consistency check: the same numbers in every document.

Output each item in order. Stop for approval of the materials before step 3.
