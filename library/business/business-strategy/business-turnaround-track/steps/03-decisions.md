# Step 3: Decisions

1. Focus: what to keep, fix, shrink or exit, from the profit-by-segment view.
2. Cost: reductions sized and timed, separating cuts that hurt the viable core from those that do not. Flag staff and contract changes for legal advice.
3. Revenue: pricing, mix and customer actions with quick payback.
4. Funding: whether the plan needs new money or lender agreement, and the options to discuss with advisers.
5. Show the effect of each decision on the 13-week cash forecast and on monthly profit.
