# Step 4: Turnaround plan

1. A 100-day plan: actions with owner, date and cash effect, in order.
2. Milestones at day 30, 60 and 100, and the trigger that means the plan is failing.
3. Stakeholder communication: staff, key customers, suppliers, lender. Honest and agreed with advisers.
4. Governance: a weekly turnaround meeting and who attends.

Sections: 100-day plan, Milestones and triggers, Stakeholder communication, Governance, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
