# Step 2: Diagnosis

1. Trace the decline: sales by product, customer and channel; gross margin; overheads, over 2 to 3 years where figures allow.
2. Separate external causes (market, competitor, costs) from internal ones (pricing, product, operations, management, debt).
3. Find which products, customers, sites or channels make or lose money after their own costs.
4. Name the core of the business worth saving, and say honestly if there may be none.

Sections: Decline in numbers, Causes, Profit by segment, Viable core, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
