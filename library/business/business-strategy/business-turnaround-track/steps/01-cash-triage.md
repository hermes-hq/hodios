# Step 1: Cash triage

1. Build a 13-week cash forecast: opening cash, receipts, payments, closing cash by week. Show the lowest point and when it hits.
2. Quick cash levers ranked by speed and harm: chase overdue debtors, sell surplus stock, pause non-essential spend, agree payment plans with key creditors or tax authorities, defer capital spend.
3. Solvency check: can debts be paid as they fall due? If not, or if close, say the owner must take insolvency advice this week.
4. Immediate controls: who approves every payment, daily cash check.

Sections: 13-week forecast, Cash levers, Solvency check, Controls, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
