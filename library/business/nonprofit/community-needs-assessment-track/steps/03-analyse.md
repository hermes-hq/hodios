# Step 3: Analyse

Make sense of what was collected. Ask for the collected data first if it has not been shared; do not proceed on assumed results.

1. Summarise who took part against who was meant to, and name which groups are under-represented and how that limits the findings.
2. For each assessment question, give the findings with their sources, separating what many people said from single strong comments, and quantitative from qualitative evidence.
3. Map assets: existing services, groups and strengths the community named.
4. List needs that emerged, rated by size (how many affected), severity, how well others already meet them, and fit with the organisation's purpose and capacity.
5. Note contradictions between sources and what would resolve them.

Sections: Who took part, Findings by question, Community assets, Needs ranked, Contradictions and limits, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
