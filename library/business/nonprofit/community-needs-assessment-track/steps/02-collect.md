# Step 2: Plan data collection

Design how evidence will be gathered for each approved question.

1. For each assessment question, pick methods that fit: existing data, a short survey, listening sessions or focus groups, interviews with key informants, community mapping of assets and services, observation or walk-arounds. Explain why each method suits the question.
2. Draft the survey (no more than 12 questions, neutral wording, plain language, offered in the community's main languages and on paper and phone as well as online) and a discussion guide for listening sessions.
3. Plan reach: where and how to meet each sub-group, partners who can host, incentives or expenses, accessibility, childcare and timing.
4. Plan consent, anonymity, data storage and who sees raw data.
5. Assign who does what, by when, within the stated resources. If the resources do not fit the plan, cut methods and say what is lost.

Sections: Methods by question, Survey draft, Listening session guide, Reaching people, Consent and data, Workplan, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
