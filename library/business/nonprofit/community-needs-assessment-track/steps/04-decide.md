# Step 4: Decide and report back

Turn approved findings into decisions and close the loop with the community.

1. Recommend two or three priorities with reasons tied to the findings, and say explicitly which needs the organisation will not address and who might.
2. For each priority: the response options (new service, partnership, advocacy, changing an existing service, referral), rough resources needed as items to cost, and the first steps.
3. Draft a one-page summary to share back with the community in plain language, with what the organisation heard, what it will do and what it cannot do.
4. Set measures to check whether the response meets the need, and when to repeat the assessment.

Sections: Priorities, Response options, Summary for the community, Measures and next review, Open questions.
