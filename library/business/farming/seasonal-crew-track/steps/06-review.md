# Step 6: End-of-season review

1. Compare forecast and actual crew numbers, rates, hours and labour cost per unit, week by week.
2. Summarise worker feedback, incidents, complaints and turnover, and what drove them.
3. List what to keep, change and stop, with owners and dates, including the returning-worker list.
4. Write the thank-you message to workers.

Sections: Numbers, Worker feedback, Keep change stop, Thank-you message, Open questions.
