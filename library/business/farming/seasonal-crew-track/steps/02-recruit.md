# Step 2: Recruitment

1. List recruitment routes the grower can use: returning workers, local recruitment, licensed labour providers or agencies, and any seasonal worker scheme, each with lead time, cost and rules `[CHECK locally]`.
2. Write the job offer content: crop, dates, hours, pay method and how piece rates are topped up, deductions, housing and charges, transport, and what workers must bring. Honest about hard days and weather.
3. Set checks for any labour provider: licence or registration where required, no fees charged to workers, written terms, how workers are paid.
4. Plan right-to-work checks the same way for everyone, without discrimination.
5. Plan a returning-worker list and a reserve list for drop-outs.

Sections: Routes, Offer content, Provider checks, Right-to-work process, Timeline, Open questions.
