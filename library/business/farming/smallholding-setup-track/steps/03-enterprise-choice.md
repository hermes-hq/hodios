# Step 3: Enterprise choice

1. List candidate enterprises that fit the land and goals (for example laying hens, a few sheep, pigs, vegetables, orchard, bees).
2. Score each: fit with the land, hours per week by season, skills needed, set-up cost to research, daily tie (who covers holidays), risk, and fit with goals.
3. Recommend starting with one or two, and say why; put the rest in a later year.

Sections: Candidates, Scoring table, Recommendation, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
