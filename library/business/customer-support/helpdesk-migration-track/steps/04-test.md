# Step 4: Test before switching

1. Write 15-25 test scenarios from real ticket types: each channel's entry point, every routing rule, VIP or tier handling, out-of-hours, reopen by a customer reply, merges, integrations showing the right customer data, satisfaction surveys, and reports.
2. For each: steps, expected result, tester, pass or fail.
3. Check a sample of imported history for correct fields, attachments and customer matching.
4. Set the go or no-go criteria for cutover.

Sections: Test scenarios (table), History check, Go or no-go criteria, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
