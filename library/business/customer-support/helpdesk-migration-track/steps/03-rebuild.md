# Step 3: Plan the rebuild

1. Order the build: fields, queues and roles first, then routing and automations, then macros, then integrations, then reports.
2. Write the routing and automation rules in evaluation order with a catch-all, and check for loops.
3. List macros to recreate with updated links and placeholders, flagging any that mention the old tool or old processes.
4. Plan training: a short guide on what changed for agents, a practice session in the new tool, and a cheat sheet for the first week.

Sections: Build order, Rules, Macros, Training, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
