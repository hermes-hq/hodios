# Step 1: Inventory the current setup

1. List every element of the current setup: channels and their entry points (support addresses, forms, chat widget, phone), queues, fields and tags, macros, automations and routing rules, service targets, integrations, reports and who uses them, user roles, and ticket history volume.
2. For each, record its owner and whether it is still used. Ask the user where usage is unknown.
3. Mark hidden dependencies: email forwarding, links to the old tool in macros or help articles, other teams' reports, integrations that write data back.
4. Decide what history to move: all, a recent period, or open tickets only, with the reason and the data rules to check.

Sections: Inventory (table: Element | Count | Owner | Used? | Dependency), History decision, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
