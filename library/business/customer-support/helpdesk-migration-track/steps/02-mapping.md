# Step 2: Map old to new

1. For each element kept, decide: move as is, clean up and move, replace with a new-tool feature, or retire. Use the move to merge duplicate macros and tags rather than copying clutter.
2. Map fields and values one to one, including statuses, priorities and customer tiers, and say how historical tickets will look after import.
3. Map users and roles, and integrations to their new connections.
4. List what the new tool cannot do the same way and the workaround or decision needed.

Sections: Mapping (table: Old | Decision | New), Field and status map, Gaps and workarounds, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
