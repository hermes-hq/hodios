# Step 4: Link it and launch

1. Map where customers get stuck to the article that answers them: product screens and error messages, checkout and order pages, order confirmation and shipping emails, the contact form (suggest articles as the customer types a subject), auto-replies and support signatures.
2. Write the link text for each placement in the customer's words.
3. Update saved replies so agents send article links for the launch topics, with a short personal line around them.
4. Launch checklist: articles verified and published, search tested with the synonyms, contact route visible, internal announcement, owner per category.
5. Baseline: record contacts per week for each launch reason (or per 100 orders) for the four weeks before launch, and how article views and searches with no results will be tracked.

Sections: Link map (table: Place | Article | Link text), Saved reply updates, Launch checklist, Baseline, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
