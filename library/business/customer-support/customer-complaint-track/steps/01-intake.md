# Step 1: Intake and facts

1. Restate the complaint in one neutral paragraph: what happened, when, to whom, and what the customer wants.
2. List every separate issue and every question the customer asked.
3. Build a timeline from the complaint and records, marking each line as customer account, business record or not yet checked.
4. Classify severity 1-4 (1 minor, 2 a failure needing a fix, 3 money lost, repeated failure or a vulnerable customer, 4 safety, illness, legal threat, regulator, data or discrimination) and list any flags.
5. Note any customer deadline and anything already promised by staff, with dates.
6. List the facts to check internally and who checks each, and send a holding reply draft if no one has answered within one working day (acknowledge, say who is handling it, give a date).

Sections: Summary, Issues and questions, Timeline, Severity and flags, Promises and deadlines, Checks, Holding reply, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
