# Step 1: Review the current menu

1. What sells and what earns: for each dish, sales, price net of sales tax, food cost and cash margin where given. Mark missing data.
2. Keep, change or drop: a first view for each dish with the reason (best seller, high margin, regulars' favourite, slow, low margin, hard to execute, seasonal ingredient ending).
3. Kitchen load: dishes that overload one station or need long prep for few sales.
4. Gaps: price points, dietary options, or styles missing for the venue's guests.
5. The brief for new dishes: how many per section, target price and food cost or cash margin, station and prep limits, seasonal ingredients to use.

Sections: Sales and margins, Keep change or drop, Kitchen load, Gaps, Brief for new dishes, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
