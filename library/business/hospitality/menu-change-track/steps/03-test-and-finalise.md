# Step 3: Test and finalise

1. Test plan: cook each shortlisted dish on the real station at service speed, timed from ticket to pass, and taste with the team; a scoring sheet for taste, look, speed and consistency.
2. Adjust from the user's test notes: recipe, portion or garnish changes, with the cost effect recalculated.
3. Final menu: dishes per section, prices (net and with tax), and dishes leaving the menu.
4. Paperwork for each final dish: spec sheet outline (quantities, pick-up steps, plating), updated allergen information to enter into the allergen matrix, and new stock lines and par levels.
5. Menu descriptions: short, accurate names and descriptions that match what is served.

Sections: Test plan, Adjustments, Final menu, Paperwork, Descriptions, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
