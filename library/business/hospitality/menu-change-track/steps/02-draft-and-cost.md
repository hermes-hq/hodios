# Step 2: Draft and cost new dishes

1. Draft each new dish against the approved brief: components, portion sizes, station, prep needed, and how it uses ingredients across the menu to limit waste and new stock lines.
2. Cost each dish: ingredient quantities, the user's purchase prices, yields (marked as estimates where no yield test exists), portion cost, food cost % at the proposed price, and cash margin. Missing prices stay `[needed]`.
3. Allergens: list the allergens in each component from the named ingredients and flag bought-in items whose labels must be checked.
4. Kitchen check: for each station, the new prep and pick-up load compared with the current menu.
5. A shortlist with one or two back-ups per section.

Sections: Draft dishes, Costing, Allergens to check, Kitchen check, Shortlist, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
