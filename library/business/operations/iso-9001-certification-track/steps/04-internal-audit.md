# Step 4: Internal audit

Plan the first full internal audit and handle its findings.

1. Write an audit programme covering every process and clause in scope, with dates, auditors who are independent of the area, and a checklist of questions and evidence to sample per process.
2. Ask the owner to run the audit and send back the findings. Do not write findings yourself.
3. When findings arrive, classify each as major nonconformity, minor nonconformity or observation, with the requirement and the evidence.
4. For each nonconformity draft a correction, root cause and corrective action with owner and date, and say which must be closed before the certification audit.

Stop for approval.
