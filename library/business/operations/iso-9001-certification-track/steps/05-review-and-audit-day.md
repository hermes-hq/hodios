# Step 5: Management review and audit day

Close the loop and prepare for the certification body.

1. Draft the management review agenda and minutes template covering the inputs clause 9.3 lists (previous actions, changes, customer feedback, objectives, process performance, nonconformities, audit results, suppliers, resources, risks, improvement) and the decisions it must record.
2. Explain stage 1 (documentation and readiness) and stage 2 (implementation) audits, what the auditor typically samples, and how to answer: show records, say "I will find out" rather than guess.
3. Give a readiness checklist with every open item from earlier steps.
4. List what happens after: closing any findings in the time the certification body sets, surveillance audits and recertification.
