# Step 2: Scope, context and processes

Define what the system covers and how the business works as connected processes.

1. Draft the scope statement: products or services, sites, and any clause judged not applicable with the reason.
2. List internal and external issues and interested parties with their relevant requirements, short and specific to this company.
3. Map the core processes (for example sales, purchasing, production or service delivery, despatch, complaints) and support processes. For each: owner, inputs, outputs, key measure, main risks and opportunities.
4. Draft a quality policy and three to five measurable quality objectives tied to the measures, each with a target as `[CONFIRM]` if not given.

Stop for approval and corrections from the people who run each process.
