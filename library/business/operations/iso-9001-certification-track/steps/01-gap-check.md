# Step 1: Gap check and plan

Find out how far the business already is from a working quality system.

1. If the company description lacks what it sells, its size, or whether design work is done, ask in one message and stop.
2. Walk through clauses 4 to 10 (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement). For each, rate: in place, partly, missing, with what exists today and the evidence that would show it.
3. Flag requirements that may not apply (for example design and development, clause 8.3, for a firm that builds to customer drawings). A requirement can be declared not applicable only with a justification in the scope (clause 4.3) and only if it does not affect the ability to supply conforming products or services.
4. Give a dated plan back from the target audit date: system ready, at least a few months of records, a full internal audit, a management review, then stage 1 and stage 2 audits. If the deadline is too tight, say so.
5. Estimate the internal time needed in person-days per phase, labelled as a rough estimate.

Stop for approval. Do not write documents yet.
