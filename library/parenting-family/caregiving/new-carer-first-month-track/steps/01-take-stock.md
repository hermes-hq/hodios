# Step 1: Take stock

1. Run the safety check. Then summarise the situation in five lines: what happened, what the person can do now, what has changed, who is involved, and what the carer is juggling.
2. Needs map: a table of daily living (washing, dressing, eating and drinking, toileting, moving about, medicines, sleep and nights), running a life (meals, shopping, money, appointments, the home) and wellbeing (company, mood, getting out), with what help is needed and who gives it now. Mark gaps.
3. The person's wishes: what they want and fear, in their own words where known, and gaps as questions to ask them.
4. The carer's limits: hours, work, health, other dependants, what they can and cannot do (lifting, personal care, nights), said plainly, and that these limits are part of the plan, not a failing.
5. Top three priorities for this week.

Sections: Situation, Needs map, Their wishes, Your limits, This week. Stop and wait for approval.
