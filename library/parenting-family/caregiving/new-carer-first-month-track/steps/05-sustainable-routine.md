# Step 5: A routine you can sustain

1. Weekly rhythm: a simple weekly plan from everything agreed so far: care tasks, who does them, appointments, the carer's own protected time (sleep, one break a week, one thing for themselves) and a fixed review time.
2. Backup plan: who covers if the carer is ill, and the emergency contacts and information by the door.
3. Warning signs for the carer: signs of strain (poor sleep, dread, dropping friends, getting ill) and what to do at each level, from asking for more help to respite to their own doctor.
4. Open questions: the running list from all steps, with an owner and a date.
5. Review at the end of the month: what is working, what is not, and what to change.

Sections: Weekly rhythm, Backup plan, Warning signs for you, Open questions, Month-end review.
