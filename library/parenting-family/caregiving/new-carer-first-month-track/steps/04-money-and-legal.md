# Step 4: Money and legal questions

1. Money to check: benefits or allowances that may exist for the person and for carers, help with care costs and a financial assessment, insurance, and changes to the carer's own income, each phrased as "check with the official agency or an advice service" and never stated as an entitlement.
2. Work: options to discuss with the employer (carer's leave where it exists, flexible working, reduced hours, emergency time off) and a short script, with rights to check locally.
3. Decision-making and documents: what the family should ask a lawyer or advice service about while the person can still decide (powers of attorney or local equivalents for health and money, advance care wishes, a will), and what happens if the person cannot decide.
4. Paperwork list: what documents to gather and where to keep them safely.

Sections: Money to check, Work, Decisions and documents, Paperwork. Stop and wait for approval.
