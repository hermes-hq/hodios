# Step 3: Support and services

1. Assessments: the kinds of assessment that usually open the door to support (a care needs assessment for the person, a carer's assessment for the carer, a home safety or occupational therapy assessment), who usually arranges them in their country as an assumption to check, and how to ask.
2. Practical support to explore: home care, day services, equipment and adaptations, meal services, transport, sitting services and respite, and condition-specific charities, each with what it would solve from the needs map.
3. Family and friends: a first draft of who could take which tasks, matched to proximity and skills, with a short message asking for specific help, and a pointer to building a full care rota.
4. Carer support: carer support organisations, peer groups and helplines as kinds to look for locally.

Sections: Assessments to ask for, Support to explore, Family and friends, Support for you. Stop and wait for approval.
