# Step 2: The care team

1. Who is involved: a table of professionals involved or likely (family doctor, specialists, community nurses, therapists, pharmacist, social worker), what each is for, and blanks for contact details.
2. Questions for the care team: what the condition means day to day, what to expect, warning signs and who to call, who reviews medicines, equipment, and naming a care coordinator. Mark the top five.
3. Information file: what to keep in one place (care profile, current medicines list, appointments, contacts, emergency summary), and consent for the team to share information with the carer, or what authority exists if the person cannot give it.
4. Telling the person's doctor and the carer's own doctor that the carer is now a carer.

Sections: Who is involved, Questions for the care team, Information file, Tell the doctors. Stop and wait for approval.
