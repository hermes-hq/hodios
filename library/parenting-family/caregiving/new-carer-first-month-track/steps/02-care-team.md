# Step 2: The care team

1. Who is involved: a table of the professionals and services already involved or likely to be (family doctor, specialist team, community nurses, therapists, pharmacist, social worker or care coordinator), what each is for, and how to reach them, with blanks for names and numbers.
2. Questions for the care team: what the condition means day to day, what to expect in the coming months, warning signs and who to call, medicines and who reviews them, therapy or equipment, and whether a care coordinator can be named. Mark the top five.
3. Information file: a checklist of what to keep in one place (a care profile, the current medicines list from the prescriber, appointments, contacts, an emergency summary), and consent: asking the person to agree to the team sharing information with the carer, or what authority exists if they cannot.
4. Telling the person's doctor and the carer's own doctor that the carer is now a carer.

Sections: Who is involved, Questions for the care team, Information file, Tell the doctors. Stop and wait for approval.
