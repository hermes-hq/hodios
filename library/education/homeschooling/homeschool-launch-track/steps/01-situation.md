# Step 1: Situation and obligations

1. Summarise each child: age, where they are in reading and maths, interests, needs, and how they feel about leaving or never starting school.
2. Summarise the family: who teaches, when, work commitments, budget, and the reasons for homeschooling in the family's own words.
3. Write the obligations checklist: questions to confirm with the official authority (notification or registration, deregistration from a current school, required subjects, records, reviews, deadlines), and who usually answers each.
4. Flag anything urgent: a child currently refusing school, a deadline, a child with additional needs who may lose support, or wellbeing concerns.

Sections: Children, Family, Obligations to confirm (table: question | who to ask | deadline if known), Urgent flags, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
