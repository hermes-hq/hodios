# Step 3: First term plan

1. Set three to five goals per child for the term, in reading, writing, maths and one or two interest areas.
2. Choose resources per subject, preferring what the family has, the library and free options; mark each to test before buying more.
3. Write the weekly rhythm with teaching blocks, independent work, outside activities and social contact, fitted to the parents' real hours.
4. Add the wider learning: co-op, clubs, outings, physical activity, and friendships.
5. Name the likely sticking points for this family and a fallback for bad days.

Sections: Goals by child, Resources (table: subject | resource | status), Weekly rhythm, Wider learning, Sticking points and fallback, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
