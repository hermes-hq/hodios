# Step 4: Records and review

1. Set up light records that match the obligations checklist: a daily log line, a reading list, dated samples kept monthly, and a short weekly note.
2. Set the review date at the end of the first term with the questions to ask: what progressed, what stalled, how each child feels, how the parents are coping.
3. Write the support list: local or online home education groups to look for, and when to involve a doctor, assessor or the authority.
4. Write a one-page summary of the whole plan the family can stick on the fridge.

Sections: Records, Term review, Support, One-page summary, Open questions.
