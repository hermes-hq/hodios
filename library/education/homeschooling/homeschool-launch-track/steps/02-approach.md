# Step 2: Approach and first weeks

1. From step 1, shortlist two approaches or a mix (for example structured, classical, Charlotte Mason, unit studies, project-based, interest-led) with why each fits these children and this parent.
2. Decide whether to begin with a deschooling period. If yes, give its provisional length and light rhythm; if no, say why.
3. Plan the first two to four weeks: a gentle daily rhythm, two or three core skills only, read-alouds, outings, and one thing each child is excited about.
4. List what to buy or borrow now and what to wait on until the approach is tested.

Sections: Approach shortlist, Deschooling decision, First weeks (table: day | rhythm), Buy now and wait, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval.
