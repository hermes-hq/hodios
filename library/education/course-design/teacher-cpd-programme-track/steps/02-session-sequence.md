# Step 2: Session sequence

1. Lay out the year's whole-staff and department sessions in order, each focused on one technique or building block, with revisits later in the year instead of new topics every time.
2. For each session: purpose, the short knowledge input (what and why), modelling (live or video), deconstruction into steps, rehearsal with feedback, and the classroom commitment staff leave with.
3. Show how department time adapts each technique to the subject, with protected time rather than an add-on.
4. Plan for early-career and support staff: what they join, and what extra or different input they get.
5. Cut or merge anything that competes with the priorities.

Sections: Year overview (Term | Session | Technique | Format | Minutes), Session outlines, Department adaptation, Different staff routes, What stops, Open questions.
