# Step 5: Assessment day plan

1. A checklist per assessment day: ID, portfolio access, equipment, room or remote setup, arrival or log-in time, contacts.
2. The night before and the morning: what to review (the criteria map, three best examples), and what not to do (new content).
3. During the assessment: ask for a question to be repeated, take a moment to choose an example, refer to the portfolio, keep to time.
4. Afterwards: when and how results usually arrive, as stated by the assessment organisation [X if unknown], and a note to reflect while it is fresh.

Sections: Checklists, Before, During, After, Open questions.
