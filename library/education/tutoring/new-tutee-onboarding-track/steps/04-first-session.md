# Step 4: First session plan

1. Plan the first session minute by minute (for a 60-minute session: about 5 rapport, 10 feedback on the diagnostic, 30 on the first priority, 10 practice, 5 wrap-up), adjusted to the session length agreed.
2. Choose the first focus to give an early, real win.
3. Write the explanation route, two worked examples, practice questions with answers and one exit question.
4. Add notes on how to give the diagnostic feedback honestly and encouragingly, and on what to note for the report.

Output sections: Timeline, Teaching notes, Practice and exit question, Notes to record.

Stop and wait for approval.
