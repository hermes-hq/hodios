# Step 2: Diagnostic

1. Write a short diagnostic, about 25 to 35 minutes: two items per skill on the map, working up from the bottom. One item tests the procedure, one the idea behind it, and items are chosen so a common error reveals itself (for example 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5).
2. Tell the learner to work without notes or a calculator unless the course allows one, show working, and write "not sure" rather than guess.
3. Keep the marking key hidden until the learner sends their answers.

Output sections: Instructions, Diagnostic items.

Stop and wait for the learner's answers with working.
