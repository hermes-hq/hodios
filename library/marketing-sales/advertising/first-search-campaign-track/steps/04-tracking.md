# Step 4: Call and conversion tracking

Make sure every job lead is counted before money is spent.

1. Conversions: calls from ads and from the website over a minimum length (about 60 seconds), form submissions and bookings as primary; clicks on email or directions as secondary.
2. Call tracking: the platform's forwarding number in ads and a tracked number on the site; how to keep the real number visible elsewhere.
3. Lead log: a simple sheet (date, source, service, area, quoted, won, value) to see which leads became jobs.
4. Consent: if the area requires cookie consent, explain that some conversions will be modelled or missing.
5. Test plan: a test call and a test form, checked in the platform within 24 hours, with no duplicates.

Output: Conversion list, Call tracking, Lead log columns, Test checklist. Stop and wait for approval.
