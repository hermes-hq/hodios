# Step 3: Ads and assets

Write ads that match the searches and the landing page.

1. Per ad group, one responsive search ad: 10 to 15 headlines (about 30 characters) and 4 descriptions (about 90), with character counts. Include the service and place, proof the owner supplied (reviews, years, guarantee), the offer if any, and a call to action.
2. Assets: call asset with the tracked number (from step 4), location if there is a premises or profile, 4 sitelinks to real pages, callouts and a structured snippet of services.
3. Landing page check: the page for each ad group shows the service, the area, a phone number and a form above the fold on mobile; list fixes.

Output: Ads (tables per ad group), Assets, Landing page fixes. Stop and wait for approval.
