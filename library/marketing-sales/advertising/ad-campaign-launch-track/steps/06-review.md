# Step 6: Seven-day review

Judge the first week from real data and decide what to change.

1. Wait for the platform export and backend results. If they are not supplied, ask for them and stop; do not estimate results.
2. Results against the brief: spend, conversions, cost per acquisition or ROAS, guardrails, and the reconciliation gap between platform and backend numbers.
3. By level: campaign, ad set and ad, with the funnel (impressions, click-through rate, landing page conversion rate, cost per result) to locate where performance breaks.
4. Creative: which angle leads and whether the difference is large enough to trust given the sample, or inconclusive.
5. Decisions: a table of Action | Where | Reason | Expected effect, covering what to pause, what to scale and by how much, what to fix, and the next creative test.
6. Learnings to record and the date of the next review.

This is the last step.
