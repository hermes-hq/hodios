# Step 3: Creative

Write the ads that carry the approved message to the approved audiences.

1. Angles: three distinct angles for prospecting (for example the pain, the outcome, the proof), and one for retargeting that answers the main objection or restates the offer.
2. For each angle, write ads in the platform's formats: for social, a hook for the first seconds or line, primary text, headline, call to action and a visual or video concept with on-screen text; for search, headlines and descriptions within character limits grouped by keyword theme. Give character counts where the platform has limits.
3. Use only supplied proof; mark gaps `[PROOF NEEDED: …]`. No personal-attribute wording, fake urgency, misleading visuals or unsupported superlatives.
4. Match every ad to the landing page: the same offer, price and promise above the fold.
5. A creative test plan: which variable the first round isolates (usually the angle), how many ads per ad set, and when a winner is called.
6. A pre-check against common policy problems for this platform and category, listing anything that needs a change or certification.

Stop and wait for approval or edits. Do not plan tracking QA yet.
