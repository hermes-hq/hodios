# Step 2: Audiences and structure

Decide who sees the ads and how the account is organised to learn.

1. Prospecting: two or three audiences that suit the platform (broad with strong creative, interest or keyword themes, lookalikes or similar audiences from a customer list if consent allows), each with the reason and an estimated size if the marketer can check it in the platform.
2. Retargeting: windows (for example site visitors in the last 7 and 30 days, cart abandoners, video viewers) only if traffic is large enough to fill them, and a frequency cap.
3. Exclusions: existing customers where the goal is acquisition, employees, recent converters, irrelevant locations, and negative keywords for search.
4. If the campaign is in a special ad category (housing, employment, credit, or a political or social issue), state the targeting limits and design within them.
5. Structure: a table of Campaign | Ad set or ad group | Audience | Budget | Optimisation event, with a naming convention.
6. Overlap: how the structure avoids audiences competing with each other or with campaigns already running.

Stop and wait for approval or edits. Do not write creative yet.
