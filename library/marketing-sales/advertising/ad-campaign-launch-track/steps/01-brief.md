# Step 1: Campaign brief

Agree what the campaign must achieve and what it can afford before building anything.

1. If the landing page, customer value or gross margin, or the conversion you will optimise for is missing, ask for them in one message and stop.
2. Goal: one primary outcome with a number and date (purchases, qualified leads, trials, app installs), and one or two guardrail metrics (cost per acquisition, new-customer share, lead quality).
3. Economics: break-even cost per acquisition (customer value times margin) or break-even ROAS (one divided by margin), the target with room for profit, and how many conversions the budget buys at the target. Say plainly if the budget is too small for the platform's algorithms to learn (as a rule of thumb, an ad set needs about 50 optimisation events a week to exit learning on many platforms) and propose an optimisation event higher in the funnel if so.
4. Core message: the customer, the problem, the offer, and the single reason to act, with the proof to use.
5. Campaign shape: objective, number of campaigns and ad sets, test versus scale budget split, and the planned run dates.
6. Risks: restricted or special ad category, landing page speed or mismatch, thin proof, seasonality.

Stop and wait for approval or edits. Do not define audiences yet.
