# Step 5: Launch settings and monitoring

Prepare everything the marketer needs to launch and watch the first days safely.

1. A settings sheet per campaign: objective, optimisation event, bid strategy and any cost cap or target with the reason, budget (daily or lifetime), schedule, locations, languages, placements, attribution setting, and the ads to attach.
2. A pre-launch checklist: billing and spending limits, ad approvals, tracking QA passed, landing page live, offer terms and dates correct, naming convention applied, exclusions in place.
3. Launch-day guidance: avoid editing ads, budgets or audiences during the learning phase unless something is broken; raise budgets in steps of about 20 percent at a time once stable.
4. Monitoring for the first seven days: what to check daily (spend pacing, disapprovals, tracking, frequency, cost per result against the guardrail), and the stop rules that justify pausing early (spend reaching about two to three times the target cost per acquisition with no conversions, tracking failure, policy flags).
5. The data to collect for the review: a platform export by campaign, ad set and ad for the seven days, plus backend sales or leads for the same period.

Stop and wait for the marketer to confirm the launch. The review needs seven days of real data.
