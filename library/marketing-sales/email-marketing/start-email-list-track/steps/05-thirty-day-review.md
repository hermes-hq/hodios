# Step 5: Thirty-day review

Review the first month with real numbers.

1. Ask for the results log, list growth by source, and the owner's time actually spent. If no numbers are supplied, ask for them and stop; do not estimate results.
2. Compute per send: click rate, bookings or orders per delivered, unsubscribe and complaint rates. Flag complaint rates above 0.1% and hard bounces above 2%.
3. Compare sign-up sources by contacts gained per week and say which to push harder.
4. Recommend three changes for the next month, at most one experiment, and whether the cadence fits the time available.

Sections: Results, List growth, What worked, Next month, Open questions.
