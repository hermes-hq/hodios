# Step 1: Triage

Work out what kind of crisis this is and how fast the organisation must move.

1. Safety first: is anyone at risk now? If so, write the safety instruction for affected people at the top.
2. Facts board: a table of confirmed facts, unconfirmed claims (including what is circulating publicly), and open questions, each with who can confirm it.
3. Severity: rate it (issue, serious, critical) by harm to people, number affected, public visibility, legal or regulatory exposure, and how fast it is spreading. Say what would raise or lower the rating.
4. Clock: what is already public, where, and how fast it is moving; when the first statement must go out (usually within the hour for anything public).
5. Team and decisions: who is on the crisis team, who approves statements, who is the spokesperson, and which advisers (legal, insurer, technical) to call now.
6. Immediate holds: scheduled marketing, posts and ads to pause.

Stop and wait for approval and any new confirmed facts.
