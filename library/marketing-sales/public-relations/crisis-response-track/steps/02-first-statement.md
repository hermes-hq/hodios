# Step 2: First statement

Say something true quickly, using the approved triage.

1. Holding statement for media and the public, about 80 to 150 words: what happened (confirmed only), care for those affected, what is being done, what affected people should do, and a committed time for the next update.
2. Staff note that reaches staff before or with the public statement: what happened, what to say if asked (refer to the named contact), what not to post, when they will hear more.
3. Short social version that links to the full statement, and a plan for replies: who monitors, which questions get a standard answer, which go to the crisis team.
4. List the phrases deliberately left out and why, and the approvals needed before release.

Stop and wait for approval. Do not release anything without it.
