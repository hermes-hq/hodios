# Step 5: After-action review

Learn from the response while memories are fresh.

1. Timeline of what happened and what was said, from the decision log and approved artifacts.
2. What worked and what did not: speed of the first statement, approval delays, accuracy (anything that had to be corrected), stakeholder order, media handling, monitoring.
3. Effect: coverage tone over time, questions and complaints volume, trust signals from customers or staff, using only data the user supplies.
4. Changes: to the crisis plan, templates, decision rights, contact lists and training, each with an owner and date.
5. Commitments made publicly during the crisis and whether each has been delivered.

Keep the review blame-free: focus on systems and decisions, not individuals.
