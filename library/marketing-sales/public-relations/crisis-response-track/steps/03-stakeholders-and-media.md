# Step 3: Stakeholders and media

Tell the right people in the right order and prepare for questions.

1. Stakeholder sequence: affected people, staff, board, regulators and authorities, insurers, key customers, partners and suppliers, investors, the local community. For each: order, who tells them, channel, and the message adapted from the approved statement. Mark notification duties to confirm with advisers.
2. Reactive Q&A: the ten to fifteen hardest questions, including hostile ones, answered only from confirmed facts, with a bridge to actions and the next update.
3. Media handling: how enquiries are logged and answered, deadlines tracked, the spokesperson's three key messages, and when to offer an interview versus a written statement.
4. Monitoring: which channels and outlets to watch, what signals mean the story is growing, and the rumours to correct with facts.

Stop and wait for approval and new confirmed facts.
