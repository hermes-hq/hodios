# Step 1: Qualify the enquiry

1. Pull out what is known: job, location, timing, budget signals, decision makers (one person, a couple, a landlord), how they found you.
2. Check fit: in your area, your kind of work, above your minimum, timing against your availability. Rate each fit, maybe or no fit.
3. Decide: visit, quote remotely (photos or video call), or decline politely with a referral.
4. Write the reply: thank them, two or three scoping questions (photos, measurements, access, timing, rough budget offered as ranges), and a visit time choice or the decline.

Sections: Enquiry summary, Fit check (table), Decision, Reply. Stop and wait for approval.
