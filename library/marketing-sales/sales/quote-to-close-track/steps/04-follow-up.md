# Step 4: Follow up

Ask what has happened since the quote went out (silence, questions, "too expensive", another quote).

1. Plan up to three touches over about three weeks, each with a useful reason: a check it arrived plus an offer to answer questions; a start date you can hold or an answer to a likely question; a friendly close-the-file note.
2. Replies for "too expensive" (ask what they are comparing, explain inclusions, offer a smaller scope or phasing, never a bare cut) and "we're still deciding".
3. When they accept: a confirmation message with deposit, start date and what happens next.

Sections: Follow-up plan (table), Messages, Objection replies, Acceptance message. Stop and wait for approval.
