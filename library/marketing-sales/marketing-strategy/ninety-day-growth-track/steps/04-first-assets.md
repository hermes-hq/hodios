# Step 4: Produce the first assets

1. Write the assets needed for the first two to three weeks of the calendar, ready to use: for example map listing description and first posts, a review request message and the moment to send it, a past-customer email or message, a partner approach note, pavement board lines, or a leaflet front and back.
2. Use only supplied facts; mark prices, dates and claims to confirm as [X].
3. Add the tracking for each asset: code, link or question, and where results are logged.
4. Write the tracking log the owner fills in weekly: date, source, enquiries, bookings or sales, notes.

Sections: Assets (one subsection per asset), Tracking per asset, Weekly log template, Open questions.

Stop and wait for approval. The next step runs when the owner returns at day 30 with the log or results.
