# Step 5: Launch review

Review the first four to eight weeks when the owner shares results.

1. Ask for the results if not provided: orders, sessions or impressions, conversion rate, advertising spend and sales, fees paid, returns, cancellations, late shipments, customer messages, and performance metrics.
2. Compare actual contribution per order with the step 1 estimate, and the time spent with what the team can sustain.
3. Diagnose: which products work, which do not, and why (visibility, price, listing quality, reviews, delivery speed).
4. Decide: expand the range, adjust (price, listings, advertising, fulfilment), or stop, with the next three actions and the date of the next review.
5. Finish with a one-page summary of decisions made through all five steps.
