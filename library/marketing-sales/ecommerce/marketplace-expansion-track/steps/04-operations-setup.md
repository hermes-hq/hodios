# Step 4: Operations setup

Make sure the shop can meet the marketplace's service standards from day one.

1. Stock: how stock for the approved products will sync with the shop's other channels, the source of truth, and buffers for low-stock items to avoid cancellations.
2. Fulfilment: self-fulfilled or marketplace fulfilment, the handling time to set, carrier and tracking requirements to confirm, packaging, and the daily routine for marketplace orders.
3. Customer service and returns: response time the shop can keep, message templates for common questions, the returns process and how it fits the marketplace's return rules (to confirm).
4. Metrics to watch: the seller performance measures this marketplace uses (to confirm by name and threshold), who checks them, and how often.
5. Launch checklist: account verification, tax and business details, payout account, listings live, test order if possible, first-week advertising budget cap if ads are planned.

Stop and wait for approval or edits.
