# Step 1: Fit and fees

Decide whether {{marketplace}} is worth it for this shop.

1. If essential facts are missing (margins, average order value, current monthly orders, how orders are fulfilled), ask for them in one short message, or state assumptions and continue.
2. Fit: who shops on this marketplace and why, how the shop's products and prices compare with what sells there, how much control the shop gives up (customer data, branding, pricing rules, returns policy), and the dependence risk if it grows to a large share of sales.
3. Costs to confirm: list every cost type (account or subscription fee, referral or commission fee, payment fee, fulfilment fee if using the marketplace's fulfilment, advertising usually needed for visibility, returns) with a placeholder for each figure and where to find it.
4. Contribution per order: using the shop's own price and cost per product and the placeholders, show the contribution per order on the marketplace next to the same order on the shop's own store.
5. Verdict: go, go with conditions, or not now, with the reason and the two or three facts that would change it.

Stop and wait for approval or edits. Do not choose products yet.
