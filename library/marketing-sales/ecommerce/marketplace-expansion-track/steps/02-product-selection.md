# Step 2: Product selection

Choose the products to launch with, based on the approved fit and fees.

1. Score each product or product group on: contribution after marketplace costs, demand and competition on the marketplace (from what the owner can see or provide), shipping size and weight, return risk, restricted-category or approval needs (to confirm), and stock depth.
2. Recommend a launch set of about 5 to 20 products, usually the strongest margin items with proven sales elsewhere, and say which to hold back and why (low margin after fees, fragile, one-offs that risk overselling).
3. Set marketplace prices: whether to match own-store prices or adjust for fees, noting that some marketplaces have price-parity expectations to confirm.
4. List what each launch product still needs: identifiers such as barcodes if required, extra photos, compliance documents, variant structure.

Stop and wait for approval or edits. Do not write listings yet.
